## My interpretation

Juan's prison history is entirely author narration, not raised in
dialogue — it colors his motivation for being on the cruise but isn't
something he's shown disclosing to anyone yet. Worth tracking closely: it's
a strong candidate for a later reveal, and whether Roier (as an old
friend) already knows this and just didn't mention it, versus genuinely
not knowing, is currently undetermined.

## Writing notes

- Voice checklist: warm, quick-witted, comfortable teasing/being teased,
  generous with people who are visibly out of their depth (see his
  handling of Foolish's Spanish).
- Things to avoid: don't play him as cynical or guarded this early — the
  source is explicit that the rivalry with Foolish doesn't exist yet at
  this point.
- Comparison anchors: EV-0001 Spanish-lesson exchange with Foolish and the
  bystander.
- Open craft questions: how/when the prison backstory surfaces, if at
  all; how the "Spanglish" merge with Foolish (mentioned by the author as
  a later development) actually builds from this starting point of Juan
  being the more linguistically confident one.
