# CH-014 — Abueloier (Roier's grandfather)

## Basic identity

- **Character ID:** CH-014
- **Full name / aliases:** Abueloier / Abueroier (spelling varies in source material — treated as the same character)
- **Species/role/archetype:** Recurring figure in The North; Roier's grandfather
- **Canon status:** Mixed — recurring presence and specific incidents are author-confirmed; his EV-0031 counseling role is QNN-dated.

## Canon facts

- Recurring character who "sometimes comes around" the North. [CANON FACT, per author]
- Reported sick/near death in the "Doctor Atomico" heart saga ([EV-0043](../01-canon-timeline/ev-0043-heart-saga.md)) — Juan, role-playing as a scientist, tries to cure him with watermelon, then potato, then a heart transplant sourced from Foolish. Abueloier's medical crisis is the pretext for the whole saga, but per the author's summary he doesn't end up actually receiving the transplant — the donated half-heart goes into a salad instead. [CANON FACT — full transcript now available]
- Acts as an impromptu counselor to Juan after the Cucurucho/Robleis reveal, advising him to talk to Robleis directly before asking Vegetta to evict him. [CANON FACT, EV-0031]
- Receives Roier's completed portrait from Juan, then advises Juan during the Graf-date crisis. He correctly recommends a direct, honest rejection, but agrees to act senile and accompany Juan when Juan cannot do it. [CANON FACT, EV-0069]
- Chaperones Graf and Juan's second date, acts as waiter, protects Juan, improvises increasingly absurd sabotage, and witnesses Juan's fear of heartbreak and extreme exclusivity confession. [CANON FACT, EV-0070]

## Personality

- Bluntly practical beneath the chaos: his first advice is simply to tell Graf the truth and leave. [CANON FACT]
- Fiercely supportive of Juan, including affirming him across sexuality/relationship expectations and insisting Graf treat “mi niño” well. [CANON FACT]
- Readily joins elaborate bits when straightforward advice fails. His sabotage is loud, bodily, contradictory, and committed. [CANON FACT]
- Can misread emotional situations: initially assumes Juan may like Graf but merely fears heartbreak, until Juan clarifies otherwise. [CANON FACT]

## Speech patterns

- Mexican Spanish with heavy slang, profanity, elongated vowels, tangents, malapropisms, and deliberately old-man phrasing. [CANON FACT]
- Talks through other people, repeats himself, and commits to improvised identities or misunderstandings without pausing (“Antonio,” weaponized senility). [CANON FACT]
- Shares a performer's vocal/comedic foundation with Roier, but should not be written as merely older Roier: Abueloier is slower, more rambling, more literal, and more old-fashioned; Roier is faster, younger, and more socially surgical. [WRITING NOTE]
