## My interpretation

Roier's memory loss (REV-0002) is entirely author narration, not shown in
dialogue — treat it as intended backstory/setup rather than confirmed
in-scene fact. It creates a specific asymmetry worth preserving in future
writing: Foolish may remember their shared history in detail (including
feeling paternal toward Roier) while Roier may not remember it clearly at
all. That gap is currently unresolved and unacknowledged by either
character on-screen.

## Writing notes

- Voice checklist: too little dialogue sample yet — keep an eye out for
  more before locking in a voice.
- Things to avoid: don't have Roier reference specific island memories
  confidently unless/until his memory loss is clarified — err toward
  vagueness or avoidance rather than sudden recall.
- Open craft questions: how much does Roier actually remember, and does
  he know he's forgetting things, or is it invisible to him?
