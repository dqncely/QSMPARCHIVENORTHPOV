# CH-010 — Q!Quackity

## Basic identity

- **Character ID:** CH-010
- **Species/role/archetype:** Loosely affiliated with [The North](../05-worldbuilding-factions/the-north.md) — doesn't live there like core members, and isn't fully considered "part of" the group by some. [MY INTERPRETATION, per author]

## Personality

- Fiercely resistant to coercive institutions: refuses Federation service even when refusal means indefinite captivity and Pepino is used as leverage. [CANON FACT, EV-0077]
- Protective toward people classified as experiments, especially when he recognizes his own treatment in theirs. [CANON FACT, EV-0078]
- Increasingly self-sacrificial: proposes carrying an implanted bomb to kill Cucurucho despite being explicitly warned he would die. [CANON FACT, EV-0078]

## Canon facts

- Got into a heated argument with Juan over not tidying his room, escalating when Quackity felt disrespected in front of his friends; left the house visibly upset. [CANON FACT]
- Later revealed (by Juan, to Roier) to have previously planned, alongside Juan, to kill Roier. [CANON FACT — as reported by Juan; Quackity's own account not yet recorded]
- Per author-provided context: feels insecure about not being considered fully part of The North; Juan comforted him about this shortly before Juan's disappearance. [MY INTERPRETATION — pending QNN confirmation]
