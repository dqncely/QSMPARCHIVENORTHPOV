# CH-023 — Fariis

## Basic identity

- **Character ID:** CH-023
- **Species/role/archetype:** Member of [The Regime](../05-worldbuilding-factions/the-regime.md).
- **Canon status:** Mixed.

## Canon facts

- Accepts a Federation task to kill Foolish in exchange for a replacement netherite sword (his own having been lost). [CANON FACT, EV-0051]
- Overhears Foolish discussing this arrangement beforehand — Foolish doesn't confront him about it directly, but knows. [CANON FACT]
- His and Foolish's deaths are both staged (by Aldo, in disguise) around the same time — real reports treat both as unexplained murders by unidentified infiltrators. [CANON FACT, EV-0051]
- Separately, catches Aldo and Cucurucho mid-conversation and is killed for real by Cucurucho for it, his belongings confiscated. Later returns (after the killing, in a separate continuity beat) to ask Aldo about his missing things, remains unconvinced by Aldo's explanation and denial. [CANON FACT, EV-0052]

## Unresolved questions

- Given he's shown dying twice in close succession (the staged death, then killed for real by Cucurucho), the exact sequence/relationship between these two deaths isn't fully clear from the source — flagged rather than resolved.
- Does his suspicion of Aldo go anywhere?
