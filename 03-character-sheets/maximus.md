# CH-020 — Maximus

## Basic identity

- **Character ID:** CH-020
- **Species/role/archetype:** North-adjacent; QSMP1 veteran, so shares that history with Foolish (and, per general North context, Tina, Roier, Vegetta). Spanish-speaking. [CANON FACT]
- **Canon status:** Mixed.

## Personality

- Deeply distrustful of the Federation, and by extension of Foolish specifically once he learns of the double-agent plan. [CANON FACT]
- Perceptive — reads Juan's defensiveness about Foolish correctly, naming it directly rather than dancing around it. [CANON FACT]
- Has an ongoing, unrelated musical side-project he pitches to Juan (studio time, a song about Foolish) — Juan deflects into an unrelated joke title ("Qsmpaz"). [CANON FACT]

## Canon facts

- Learns from Juan about Foolish's Federation double-agent plan; strongly disapproves, doesn't trust Foolish not to have already betrayed them. [CANON FACT, EV-0045]
- States directly to Juan that he believes Juan likes Foolish, and that this explains his "irrational hatred" toward him — Juan denies it aloud but is visibly shaken by it internally afterward. [CANON FACT, EV-0045]
- Later brings up Juan's disclosure directly to Foolish, making clear he doesn't trust him — this is how Foolish finds out Juan told him. [CANON FACT, EV-0046]
