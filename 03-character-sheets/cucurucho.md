# CH-012 — Cucurucho

## Basic identity

- **Character ID:** CH-012
- **Species/role/archetype:** Enigmatic recurring figure; identity itself is disputed
- **Canon status:** Deliberately unresolved — the entire point of this entry is that "Cucurucho" as experienced by Juan may not be a single consistent identity.

## Canon facts

- Was expected on a date with Juan; multiple, mutually conflicting contemporaneous reports exist about whether he showed up at all. [CANON FACT — the conflicting reports themselves are real; which account is "true" is not resolved]
- When he did appear (per one account), witnesses said he looked and acted differently than usual, raising doubt about whether it was "the real one." Q!Robleis vanished from the island at that exact moment. [CANON FACT]
- A QSMP TCG card later reveals that Juan's romantic Cucurucho encounters involved Q!Robleis in disguise, meaning the relationship Juan believed he had with the real Cucurucho did not exist as he understood it. [CANON FACT, EV-0031]
