# CH-026 — Dark Cucurucho

## Basic identity

- **Character ID:** CH-026
- **Species/role/archetype:** An entity distinct from [Cucurucho](cucurucho.md) — shares his voice (distorted) and apparent likeness, but not established as the same being. Previously referred to in the tracked timeline only as "the entity" or "Fatal Error."
- **Canon status:** Mixed — his existence and the ritual are [CANON FACT]; his exact relationship to Cucurucho is genuinely unresolved.

## Canon facts

- Posts build instructions for a temple (spanning bedrock to sky) at a remote location, requiring the builder to summon him and surrender their soul upon completion. [CANON FACT, EV-0055]
- Speaks to Foolish once he places the first block, confirming him as the one who will build the temple. Requires a loyalty oath, willingness to die, and binds Foolish's soul to himself until the temple is finished — explicitly stating he can't trust human mortals otherwise. [CANON FACT, EV-0055]
- Has no apparent sense of humor — a nervous joke from Foolish doesn't land. [CANON FACT]
- States that Foolish's absence from the North will feel like weeks to them but immediate to Foolish, and that a "replacement" has already been arranged for his absence. [CANON FACT]

## Unresolved questions

- Is Dark Cucurucho the real Cucurucho corrupted/split, an unrelated entity that's taken his face and voice, or something else entirely? Not resolved — flagged rather than assumed identical to or separate from [Cucurucho](cucurucho.md).
- What/who is the "replacement" for Foolish?
- Was "Fatal Error" — the entity Foolish had been chasing for days — Dark Cucurucho all along, or a separate phenomenon that led him here? Not confirmed.
