## Each person's perception

Graf sees Juan as beautiful, punctual, dedicated, caring toward the North, and worth treating gently. He explicitly refuses to accept Foolish's joking description of Juan as a scumbag. [CANON FACT]

Juan sees Graf as kind and as a friend. His repeated avoidance comes from not wanting to break Graf's heart, not from reciprocated attraction. The author frames this as a crisis of conscience: Juan understands consuming love too well to accept it dishonestly. Trust is friendly but asymmetrical. Graf is romantically open; Juan is protective of Graf's feelings but unable to set a clear boundary. The central conflict is not hostility but Juan's avoidance, compounded by prior romantic trauma and the hidden Foolish/Juan tension. [STRONG INFERENCE]
