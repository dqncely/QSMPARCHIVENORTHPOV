## What Q!Foolish knows about Q!Juan

- His fluency/comfort code-switching Spanish/English, his role in the North, his temper, his capacity for real manipulation when he suspects a lie (EV-0009), his rare sincerity in private (EV-0028), and — as of EV-0030 — enough trust to bring him into Federation-related risk.

Foolish does not yet know (per tracked canon) the details of Juan's romantic history with the fake Cucurucho, or the full weight of what Juan told him in their final EV-0033 conversation before he disappeared (not recorded).

## What Q!Juan knows about Q!Foolish

- That Spanish isn't his first language; that he's capable of real betrayal for personal gain (EV-0009) but also real loyalty/protection (EV-0013); that he has a genuine history with the Federation and QSMP1 (EV-0030); that his claims of innocence aren't always reliable (EV-0017/EV-0018 contrast).

Juan does not yet know Foolish's private resolve not to get attached ([REV-0004](../11-continuity-tracking/rev-0004-foolish-private-resolve.md)) or the full truth behind his island/purgatory history ([REV-0001](../11-continuity-tracking/rev-0001-foolish-purgatory-history.md)).
