# Q!Juan and Q!Aldo

## Relationship summary

Juan and Aldo begin as complementary leaders within The North: Juan maintains the house, rituals, and social structure while Aldo serves as Generalísimo and handles military threats. Juan trusts Aldo's authority and Aldo's Federation task uses Juan as the leverage most likely to force his compliance. [CANON FACT]

## Dynamic

Juan's loyalty to Aldo survives evidence that Aldo has changed: he prioritizes restoring Aldo's mind and rank, then later seeks his guidance. Aldo's earlier willingness to surrender for Juan makes the later coercion especially destabilizing. [CANON FACT for behavior; MY INTERPRETATION for emotional weight]

Their conflict should not be flattened into simple betrayal. Aldo has experienced captivity and brainwashing, and the available QNN reports do not establish how much of his later behavior is chosen, coerced, strategic, or altered. Juan also approaches the conflict through his own rigid expectations about family loyalty and work. [CANON FACT for unresolved agency; MY INTERPRETATION for framing]

## Current state

As of EV-0073, their old hierarchy still shapes Juan's behavior, but mutual trust is badly compromised. Juan attempts private resolution; Aldo continues treating him as a debtor and business target. [CANON FACT]
