*(They talk further about Aldo — Tina's reaction mirrors Foolish's — and about her new role as Generalísima, and needing Aldo back.)*

Juan feels an elation he hasn't in a while: he's back, Aldo's back
(nominally), Foolish is back, Tina is back — everyone who'd gone missing
