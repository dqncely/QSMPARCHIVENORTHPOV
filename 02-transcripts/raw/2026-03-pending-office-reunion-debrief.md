## Author's note

This is a real turning-point scene: both admit, in different registers,
that they missed the office and each other specifically. Juan admits —
for the first time to anyone — that he's afraid to go outside; when
others asked him via communicator to go out, he'd always deflect with
"later." Over the following days he goes outside more, mostly to work on
his new art shop, but the fear is established as real and specific to
him alone until this moment. Foolish inviting him on a solo camping trip
reads, per the author, as something close to a date.
