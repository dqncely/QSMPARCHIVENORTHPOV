## Content

Foolish's "replacement" during his absence (per Dark Cucurucho's promise
in [EV-0055](../../01-canon-timeline/ev-0055-dark-cucurucho-ritual.md))
was, functionally, Foolish's body without him in it — possessed or
soulless, seen by several members around the server during the week.
Katie B. had her wedding to Tomate at this time, held at Erwoon's church;
Juan attended (he always wears armor when outside the North now, feeling
unsafe otherwise). "Foolish" was present at the wedding too, but
motionless and non-verbal — when Juan spotted him he stopped singing
mid-song and approached, calling his name, then trying to provoke a
reaction by calling him "monster." Nothing worked. Still, when Federation
workers were spotted at the wedding and Juan grew anxious, he sat beside
"Foolish" for comfort anyway.

More days pass with Foolish still gone. Aldo, meanwhile, is deeply
brainwashed — repeating "enjoy the island" constantly, wildly out of
character. When Juan finally gets to see him and tries to talk sense into
him, Aldo hurts him, framing it as what Juan wanted if he insists on
staying against the Federation. Tina, similarly shaken after reuniting
with Juan, leaves shortly after to search for Aldo herself. She doesn't
find him — instead gets knocked out and is brought back to the mansion
alongside a group of "Nachos," chaotic, destructive child-like entities
with Cucurucho's face (comparable to the QSMP1-era egg children), who had
also returned and been attacked by Aldo.

Juan's priorities going forward: get Aldo restored to Generalísimo (he
stepped down on his own return), and wait for Foolish to come back from
"camping."
