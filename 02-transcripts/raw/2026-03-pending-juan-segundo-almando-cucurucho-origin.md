## Summary

By this point, Juan had already been named "Second in Command" /
"Segundo Almando" by Vegetta — one of the earliest formal titles handed
out, earned largely through the strenuous labor of collecting sandstone
(arenisca) and hollowing out the mountain where the mansion would be
built.

*(Side note from the author: a lot of fan art depicts Juan as fairly
twinkish, but given the labor he did in the first weeks on the island,
he's built up some real mass — though nowhere close to Foolish's inhuman
build.)*

With Foolish's addition to the household, and the hierarchy filling in —
Aldo as "generalísimo," Molly as princess, Roier as prince — the North's
internal dynamic was slowly forming. Alongside this came a number of
pets, some outranking human members socially: Estupidog (the dog), and
Cucurucho — still mysterious, especially to newcomers like Juan.

Juan, despite himself, took a liking to that mysteriousness. He ignored
that Cucurucho treated everyone (including him) with the same
indifference — he was convinced there was something there. Why else
would Cucurucho bring him a flower? Other North members, Tina and Foolish
in particular (who'd recently started getting on his nerves), read this
as a malformed obsession; Juan figured it stemmed from jealousy on their
part.

Juan is not casual about a new love — he'd call for Cucurucho over the
communicator, even in public chat, running over shouting affectionately
for "Cuqui" any time he spotted the bear. He set up a date for the two of
them and waited all day.

*(This waiting-all-day setup leads directly into the "date" transcript —
see [2026-03-pending-robleis-as-cucurucho-first-date.md](2026-03-pending-robleis-as-cucurucho-first-date.md).)*
