## Transcript — Scene 1: Mansion construction begins; first island meeting

Vegetta brings along some friends (affectionately, "his slaves") to help
build his vision: a giant multi-room mansion. Whoever helped build would
have a place ("faction") in it. He brings his "adopted" kids — Aldo,
Molly, Juan, and a few others — to the chosen spot. By this point the
mansion is in its early stages: foundation and walls up, not complete.

Foolish had talked with Vegetta a couple of days before about living in
the mansion; Vegetta offered him a place given their history on the first
island. Foolish had also started his own build a few km away — a giant
purple dragon, where he planned to make kelpamine.

Juan had been so preoccupied helping with the mansion that he'd forgotten
there were people outside it. He walks into the chest room and spots a
figure talking to himself. The figure looks familiar, but Juan can't
place him.

> **J:** Hola!

That catches Foolish off guard; he turns around immediately.

> **F:** Hello-? Oh, hello hello!

Juan figures it might be good to step outside his comfort zone.

> **J:** Hi, how are you?
> **F:** Oh, I'm good, I'm good. How are you?
> **J:** I'm good, I'm good..

(Juan echoes the words, feeling how they sit on his lips.)

> **J:** I'm looking the new house
> **F:** Ohhh, we're gonna be living in luxury!
> **J:** Hehe… Yes, uh, where do you live?
> **F:** Oh, um- so i'll be like.. I'll be living here,

(He walks to the uncompleted wall — glass will go there eventually.)

> **F:** But then I'm also building an extra little thing-
> **J:** ohh..
> **F:** -It's where I'm gonna produce all my drugs
> **J:** Okay.

Foolish walks outside, expecting Juan to follow.

> **F:** It's right down here. And then.. On that island up ahead.

(He points to a rough, half-built structure — mostly dirt, but the shape
is there. A dragon.)

Juan looks in awe, switching to Spanish — still not fully comfortable
reacting in English.

> **J:** Oh, es un dragon!
> **F:** Si, si, si si!

(Foolish nods, proud but not smug.)

> **J:** Goood, I love it! Nice!

(The "d" sound in "good" is almost silent with his accent.)

> **F:** Thank you, Thank you!

## Transcript — Scene 2: First kelpamine sample

Foolish gives Juan a bottle of kelpamine — his first official "sale,"
though it cost nothing; he was just sampling, testing the waters. It was
mostly convenience — Juan was the first person he ran into after
finishing his very first batch.

> **Foolish:** "Here, you can have this now, or later."

(He hands Juan a small bottle of glowing green liquid. The label reads
something Juan can't quite make out.)

> **Juan:** "Oh.. K-Kelp-?"

(Juan inspects it closely for about ten seconds.)

> **Juan:** "I gonna try right now."

(This surprises Foolish slightly.)

> **Foolish:** "Yeah, yeah, it's gonna be like an out of body experience, oh-"

(Juan drinks, and disappears — invisibility being one of the main side
effects.)

> **Foolish:** "-Yeah, you dont even have a body anymore."

(Juan feels dizzy, floaty.)

> **Juan:** "I-I'm flying in the world.. I'm flying, I'm flying-"

(Said almost in a whisper, quieter with each word.)

> **Foolish:** "Soon I'll be giving this to everyone."

Juan just keeps repeating that he's flying. He wasn't a fan of the
experience — not like other North members (Aldo, notably, consistently
buys out Foolish's supply). Juan didn't like the feeling, and had no
enemies to spy on, so it was practically useless to him. Still, an
interesting experience.
