- **Date:** Unknown — the day after [EV-0067](../../01-canon-timeline/ev-0067-graf-first-date.md)
- **Link/channel:** Not provided
- **Language:** Spanish and English; Graf also speaks Polish elsewhere
- **Coverage:** Dialogue is retained as supplied; passages the author supplied as summaries remain prose rather than being converted into invented dialogue. Writer interpretations are separated below.

## Source notes

Juan spends the day painting Q!Roier in a composition loosely inspired by *The Birth of Venus*. Graf repeatedly visits or messages about the promised second date. Foolish never arrives to perform the backup plan. Why Foolish is absent is **not canonically established in this source**: being distracted at the temple, playing a game in his office, or being detained by Dark Cucurucho are possible writing solutions only.

Katie knows Graf has a date but knows the date only by the alias “Juanita.” She supplies Graf with a large white bow for the presumed girl and wants to spy. Juan does not know anyone named Juanita.
