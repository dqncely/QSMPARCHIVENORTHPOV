Left alone, still very drunk, Juan and Foolish end up on the couch in
Juan's room — closer together than strictly necessary — neither quite
ready to stop being around the other despite having spent the whole day
together already. Per author: this is the natural response to being
apart from your best friend for that long, even though neither of them
has ever said "best friend" outside of earlier jokes. Foolish helps clean
the cast residue off Juan's arm, though it doesn't go smoothly given
their state; both say things that night they won't remember in the
morning.
