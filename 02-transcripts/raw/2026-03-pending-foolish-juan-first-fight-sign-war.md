## Transcript

Juan sighs, getting up from the chair in his still minimally-decorated
new office (three chairs, a table, a couch), and looks dramatically out
the window.

> **Juan:** "ay… ay, ay. Foolish, Foolish, Foolish."

Foolish, already taking great pleasure in this:

> **Foolish:** "Yeah?"

Juan takes a dramatic breath and sits back down.

> **Juan:** "ay, ay ay ay.. Foolish. You know that I am second in command plus, right?"
> **Foolish:** "Yeah, I've heard."
> **Juan:** "Yeah. Uh, En mi trabajo, como segundo almando plus, yo me encargo de la escritura de los letreros." *(So that's what this was about — the name cards Juan put up outside each member's room, with name and house rank.)* "Me de cuenta que te tomaste la libertad cambiar el letrero de tu habitación."

This sends Foolish into a fit of laughter. He replies as seriously as he can manage:

> **Foolish:** "oh yeah, it's just like- the sign broke. So I needed to.. to move it."
> **Juan:** "sí, y pusiste en el letrero.. 'Second in Command'.."
> **Foolish:** "oh yeah, well you know. I just think I'm kind of second in command."
> **Juan:** "sí, pero no pediste un autorización para cambio de letrero. sabes que hay un trámite para esto, no?"

Foolish feels his grin grow before he can stop it.

> **Foolish:** "oh my apologies…I didn't realize you were second in command, plus in charge of the signs."
> **Juan:** "Si.. Si, ay..Foolish..Foolish, Foolish"

Juan sighs and gets back up; Foolish smiles more openly now that Juan's back is turned.

> **Foolish:** "No, this is a great meeting- This is a great meeting."
> **Juan:** "Ah, pero.." *(turns, leans on the table)* "Tengo una mala noticia para ti."
> **Foolish:** "Bad news? What?"
> **Juan:** "sí, ya que tomaste una decisión equivocada.. tengo que degradarte a tercero al mando."
> **Foolish:** "WHAT?"

Foolish slams his hands on the table, scandalized, and gets up; Juan matches his energy.

> **Juan:** "NOT my decision, is just part of a process, Foolish."

Foolish, having fun, wants to see how far he can push it.

> **Foolish:** "There's no way! Thats it, I- I'm gonna.. No, I'm mad-"

*(He breaks a glass panel behind Juan's office.)*

> **Foolish:** "There, I broke your window-"
> **Juan:** "Foolish! OKAY, FOURTH IN COMMAND! FOURTH!"

They begin yelling over each other — neither knowing this would repeat countless times going forward.

> **Foolish:** "FOURTH? FOURTH IN COMMAND? AFTER ALL THE WORK IVE DONE?"
> **Juan:** "YOU FUCKING BROKE THE FUCKING WINDOW"
> **Foolish:** "OH? Oh LETS SEE HOW FAR WE CAN GO." *(breaks another panel)*
> **Juan:** "AHH, OKAY, QUINTO, QUINTO AL MANDO!"
> **Foolish:** "OKay.. Okay-yeah-"
> **Juan:** "-AHORA ERES EL MALDITO QUINTO AL MANDO! A NADIE LE IMPORTA EL QUINTO AL MANDO, AHORA TU ERES ESE-"
> **Foolish:** "-WHATEVER, WHATEVER, That dog still ranks higher than you, chinga tu madre!"

Foolish storms out. Juan demands:

> **Juan:** "VETE DE MI OFICINA."

And follows him out anyway.
