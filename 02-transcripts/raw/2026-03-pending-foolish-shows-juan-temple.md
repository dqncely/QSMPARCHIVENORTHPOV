## Notes

Same mixed-source convention as prior entries. **This scene reverses the
earlier assumption that Juan doesn't know about the Dark Cucurucho
pact** — flagged and updated, not silently changed. See
[REV-0010](../../11-continuity-tracking/rev-0010-juan-learns-dark-cucurucho-pact.md).
Also worth noting: the risk of losing his soul is described here by
Foolish as still conditional/future ("once the temple gets built... I
have to give up my soul"), which sits alongside — not necessarily
contradicts — [EV-0055](../../01-canon-timeline/ev-0055-dark-cucurucho-ritual.md)'s
framing of his soul as already "bound... until my temple is complete."
Read as: bound now, permanently forfeited only at completion. Not fully
resolved; flagged.
