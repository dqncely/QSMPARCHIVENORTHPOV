## Transcript

Context, per author: there's been ongoing tension since arrival, mostly
with the newer Regime faction (Ash, Tubbo, possibly Haiper and others).
Juan tries to keep peace between factions despite his colleagues having
different ideas. He's walking the mansion looking for something to fix
when:

> **Ash:** "Where's the fucking trai-"

He doesn't finish — Juan jumps back, startled by his sudden, menacing presence.

> **Juan:** "AH- What are you doing here!?" *(voice trembling — he has no resources to defend himself)*
> **Ash:** "The train is missing from the regime, where the fuck is it!?" *(pulls out his axe)* "Where did they take it?!"
> **Juan:** "la WHAT To the WHAT??" *(already backed against the wall)*
> **Ash:** *(just stares)*
> **Juan:** "Wha- I- We don't know I just- We don't- We don't stole anything!" *(shaking his head aggressively)*
> **Ash:** "Where are they? They're not here. You said they would be here, and they're not here. Aldo waystoned away just now."
> **Juan:** "A-Aldo don't do anything I-I swear that Aldo don't stole you.. Okay?"
> **Ash:** *(laughs)* "They stole our train?"
> **Juan:** "Eh- No- Okay-"
> **Ash:** "Is that what that was? The talk of peace was a distraction-"
> **Juan:** "Thats not true!! I just forgor, that's not true! eh.. uh- Let me ask Wh-Where they are! Okay?" *(pulls out his communicator)*
> **Ash:** *(laughing)* "You ask them where the train is."
> **Juan:** "No, no-"

Juan spots Foolish at the mansion entrance and runs over, relieved.

> **Juan:** "FOOLISH, FOOLISH!-"
> **Foolish:** "-JUAN! Juan! I got the train! I got the train!"

Juan's face drops.

> **Juan:** "MOTHERFUCKER- OKAY- I SWEAR TO GOD I DONT SAY-"

Ash is already cackling.

> **Ash:** "WHAT TRAIN ARE YOU TALKING ABOUT?"
> **Foolish:** "You said to take the train!?"
> **Juan:** "I- FOOLISH? Foolish!?? I'm not- FOOLISH!" *(mortified, visibly considering homicide)*

Foolish ends up clarifying he was joking — he'd overheard Ash and Juan talking about a missing train and got a terribly evil idea. *(Per author: whether Foolish had actually taken the train and used the "joking" claim to walk it back, or never had it at all and just wanted to see the reaction, isn't resolved by this scene.)*
