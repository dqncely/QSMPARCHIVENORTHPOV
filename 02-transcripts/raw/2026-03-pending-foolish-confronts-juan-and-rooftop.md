## Transcript — Scene 1: the confrontation

> **Foolish:** "The more people that know, The tougher- the tougher it is. To make sure it stays a secret—"
> **Juan:** "Yeah, yeah yeah. It's a problem but dont wo-"
> **Foolish:** "You didn't even wait a day!"
> **Juan:** "I believe in fucking Maximus! Okay?! Relax. Maybe he's.. Useful."
> **Foolish:** "He could he but also, he might make things tougher. He hates the federation!"
> **Juan:** "yea- I'm sorry, I'm sorry! Okay?"
> **Foolish:** "Oh my Goodness.. You know what? Just take your- just take your little heart."

*(He walks out, mumbling purposefully loud enough for Juan to hear.)*

> **Foolish:** "Stupid little heart"
> **Juan:** "No- What the-"

*(Juan follows him out.)*

> **Foolish:** "Yeah, just have your little heart."
> **Juan:** "Hijo de la verga-" *(muttered)* "No." *(throws it back at him)*

*(Foolish chuckles nervously, unsure how to respond. Juan's eyebrows slightly unfurrow from the fake-angry look he always wears when Foolish does something "totalmente irritante." It almost looked like..)*

> **Foolish:** "Heh.. Okay then, I want thirty emeralds."
> **Juan:** "Fuck you!"

## Transcript — Scene 2: the rooftop ("cacahuatazo")

*(A bit later. Juan goes looking for Foolish at his dragon.)*

> **Juan:** "Hey."
> **Foolish:** "Hello.." *(tone shifted to sound mad)*
> **Juan:** "You look great!" *(walking up the dragon entrance)*
> **Foolish:** "Oh. Thanks." *(sarcastic)*
> **Juan:** "Heh, Yeah. That's amazing!"

*(Foolish just nods, sits, waits.)*

> **Juan:** "Fuck- So.. cacahuatazo?" *(their codeword for discussing the Federation)*
> **Foolish:** "Okay. cacahuatazo. Yeah. Okay, first of all once again. You idiot for telling Max!"
> **Juan:** "Yeah, yeah. I fucked up."
> **Foolish:** "He's like the most anti federation guy in the world!"
> **Juan:** "Yeah, yeah yeah I don't know.. I just forgot that- I was thinking that maybe is gonna work.. But no- What a terrible idea, fuck."
> **Foolish:** "Okay no, listen. I'm already thinking we can clean up that loose end. I think, you know. In the next couple days I can just say I didn't get the job because my cover was blown, and then we go back to just the core group of people that know!"
> **Juan:** "Yeah, yeah yeah yeah! That's great!"
> **Foolish:** "Okay, so-"

*(Directly afterward, Foolish asks to move somewhere more discreet.)*

> **Foolish:** "maybe a different location, follow me?—"

*(They teleport somewhere high up.)*

> **Foolish:** "Alright, so. I got asked today by the federation worker, if there was anybody else looking to get a job. And i.. Threw out your name"

*(Juan pauses, then, tentatively:)*

> **Juan:** "..What do you think?"

*(Foolish, excited:)*

> **Foolish:** "hehee.. I think it could be a great idea."

*(Juan looks out at the infrastructure below, pebble-small from this height.)*

> **Juan:** "I dont know, Foolish. All this situation is so fucked up.. Maximus is so angry.. uh- Aldo is so confused. I dont know i- -I'm scared"

*(Foolish just smiles.)*

> **Foolish:** "It's time to put your- pantalondes grandes- grande on!"

*(The phrase flies over Juan's head — some metaphors don't translate.)*

> **Juan:** "i uh- a size more bigger for my pants?"
> **Foolish:** "no no- you need to out on your big pants on—!"
> **Juan:** "i know my size is okay i- im not getting fat, im okay, im fit! I like gym.. I drink cratin-"
> **Foolish:** "No, okay.. it was just a metaphor."
> **Juan:** "Oh, okay, okay.. but I can stay with this pants?" *(best puppy-dog face)*

*(Foolish pauses, looks away to hide a growing grin — and maybe something else.)*

> **Foolish:** "You can keep those pants although.. if you take the job, you might get another pair of pants, you know?"
> **Juan:** "i dont get it.."
> **Foolish:** "like a-, like a uniform"

*(Juan lights up.)*

> **Juan:** "oh, yeah oh, yeah!"
> **Foolish:** "and you love uniforms!"

*(Juan thinks a moment.)*

> **Juan:** "you think maybe if i work with these guys.. i can help the north..?"
> **Foolish:** "there a chance, that's what we're here to find out."
> **Juan:** "If i can help the north, its okay."
> **Foolish:** "thats the goal, okay? that is the goal." *(making sure Juan understands this is for their benefit)*
> **Juan:** "yeah, yeah."

*(Foolish explains the plan.)*

> **Foolish:** "we become coworkers. at the federation we do everything in our power to gain knowledge or any advantage, and badabing badaboom"
> **Juan:** "and nobodys gonna knows that i work with the federation?"
> **Foolish:** "well thats what we need to figure out."
> **Juan:** "yeah, i expect that nobody knows."
> **Foolish:** "do- do we just leave it to us.. do we include aldo, tina? do we include.. no one?"

*(Juan answers faster than Foolish expects.)*

> **Juan:** "no one, yeah! maybe aldo and tina. they can understand maybe."
> **Foolish:** "yeah but honestly it might be a good back up layer, some people know about me- but nobody knows about you."
