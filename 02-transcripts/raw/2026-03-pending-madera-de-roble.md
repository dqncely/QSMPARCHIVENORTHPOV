## Transcript

Foolish waltzes into Juan's office, called in by communicator a couple
minutes earlier — something about Vegetta or Aldo needing supplies.

> **Foolish:** "Okay what does he need?"
> **Juan:** "Oak wood"
> **Foolish:** "wood?"

Juan repeats it in Spanish so Foolish can learn.

> **Juan:** "madera de roble"
> **Foolish:** "roble?"
> **Juan:** "Si. we need madera de roble"
> **Foolish:** "madere wait what is madere?"
> **Juan:** "madera is wood. roble, wood roble."
> **Foolish:** "oh tree im thinking of the word tree, is um.. arbol, arvol?"
> **Juan:** "yeah aRbol. but we need madera."
> **Foolish:** "mm.. madera"
> **Juan:** "yeah, madera."
> **Foolish:** "madera roble.. roble madera.."
> **Juan:** "—yeah we need a lot"
> **Foolish:** "..madera de roble"
> **Juan:** "si, madera de roble"
> **Foolish:** "modera de roble"

Juan rolls his eyes, emphasizing the "a" in madera.

> **Juan:** "madera"
> **Foolish:** "nesesictm.. ti temos.."

Juan holds back a giggle fit before Foolish finally gets it.

> **Foolish:** "..necesitamos."
> **Juan:** "si necesitamos,"
> **Foolish:** "ah, necesitamos roble de madera."
> **Juan:** "no, al revés, necesitamos madera de roble."
> **Foolish:** "necesitamos roble.. wait no—"

*(Juan suspects he's doing this on purpose.)*

> **Juan:** "FUCK! madera de roble"
> **Foolish:** "madera de roble! necesitamos."
> **Juan:** "yeah, yes yes. please."
> **Foolish:** "perfecto. ahora?"
> **Juan:** "yes."
> **Foolish:** "no."

*(Foolish giggles, and they both dissolve into laughter.)*

> **Juan:** "why the hell no??"
> **Foolish:** ".. uno bromita. una bromita."
> **Juan:** "thank you, now go to work!" *(switches on the office alarm briefly)*
> **Foolish:** "thats what i was just about to do, I just said i was about to go to work, you dont have to tell me to go to work! im already going! yo voy—"
> **Juan:** "Okay, go to work."
> **Foolish:** "—trabajo!"
> **Juan:** "Go to work, yeah, go to work."

Foolish heads out to get the wood, almost without thinking about it.
