## Content — the spiral begins

Per author: Foolish starts taking more kelpamine than usual and spending
more time in the forest, avoiding thinking about Juan's absence, bringing
along anyone willing to keep him company — though nobody manages to
understand him the way Juan does. He camps with Aldo, Tina, and Mike at
different times; each mentions, offhand, how much he's drinking (alcohol
and/or kelpamine), and that he should cut back — he keeps saying "did you
guys hear that?" and acting paranoid. The entity does appear a couple
times, genuinely terrifying him, but he keeps going back anyway, starts
recording to prove it's real. At some point the entity starts repeating
"Juan" and sends coordinates — later revealed as Aldo pranking him to get
him to come back to the castle, but it only pulls Foolish deeper into
searching, unsure by the end what's real.

**Campfire, after they'd actually encountered the entity together** (Aldo
hadn't believed it existed until he saw it attacking Foolish, then jumped
straight into general-mode action). Aftermath, still on edge, trying to
stay lighthearted:

> **Aldo:** "I really miss Juan. He doesn't do drugs like you, Foolish"
> **Foolish:** "Well... sometimes he does"
> **Tina:** "I miss Juan too. I haven't seen him since the meeting"
> **Foolish:** "Yeah, i can't believe he got kidnapped. Oh, wait- we forgot to say that part.."
> **Tina:** "He's kidnapped???"
> **Aldo:** "yeah, the federation kidnapped him."
> **Foolish:** "I just got really distracted-"
> **Tina:** "What do you mean you got distracted?? That's literally your boss, you don't-"
> **Foolish:** "No- I no i did- Well like- Listen w-we I-"

## Content — alone in the forest

Foolish spends nights out there alone, occasionally visited by Tina,
Aldo, Roier, Mike. He talks to his camera like it's a person, stumbles
around calling for "Fatal Error" (the entity). Others drift off eventually
— bored, homesick. Tina keeps asking if he's on drugs and if that's why
he can stay out so long; he insists he comes out with a clear mind, she
says he's just performing for the camera. A running bit: Foolish says
"did you hear that?", Tina says "no, dude.. are you okay?" One night Mike
shows Tina empty kelpamine bottles he found in the grass; they ask
Foolish about it, he denies it, claims he'd save any he had for
recycling.

*(Author's Mike character notes: outgoing, lively, good-natured, sometimes
talks more than he should. Prone to daring/reckless behavior, sometimes
encourages violence from others when given the chance. Likes causing
chaos despite being fairly quiet one-on-one. Gets into trouble a lot but
hates upsetting people — has real courage underneath it.)*

Around this time, Foolish gets a sharp, unfamiliar chest pain while
working at his kelp dragon — he assumes someone nearby used a totem
(anytime someone does, it hurts him too, being part-totem), and jokes
aloud, to no one, asking who almost died. *(Author's open question: does
the earlier heart-exchange — see [EV-0043](../../01-canon-timeline/ev-0043-heart-saga.md)
— mean Foolish now feels something of Juan's pain, given he ate half of
Juan's heart and Juan ate half of his? Not resolved; flagged as an open
plot question rather than decided here.)*

## Content — the temple and Dark Cucurucho

Foolish eventually gets coordinates — `-666,66,666` — hours away by
elytra. His first attempt ends when he's hit by kelpamine's rare (1%) bad
side effect and dies, forcing him to restock and start over. He finds an
unfinished, demonic-looking temple with posted instructions around a
cave — exactly the kind of unsettling he'd been looking for. By this
point, with Aldo gone and Juan still missing, he feels he has nothing
left to lose.

> **Instructions (posted at the site):**
> "1. Build Temple. Do not remove existing blocks. Temple must touch Sky and Bedrock at least 1 block. We will take over spawn as soon as you get started.
> 2. Make way-stone to your island.
> 3. Summon me.
> 4. Surrender your soul to me."

He decides to take the project on — building is something he loves
(shared with Vegetta, no less), and the "surrender your soul" line is
the only part that unsettles him. He's died countless times, given up
half his heart, had his DNA taken unwillingly — but never his soul, as
far as he can remember. He decides to do it anyway. *Nothing to lose,
right? Maybe it gets his friends out.*

Before leaving, he leaves a book in his room:

> **Book (in Foolish's room):**
> "Dear North, I will be away for at least a week But don't worry! I will be busy camping looking for more information on the Fatal Error it IS very important. I have left instructions to Sapotter on how to take care of the house if Juan has still not returned. If I am gone for more than 2 weeks. assume the monster ate me. GG *(laughing with tears emoji)* — Juan es un pendejo."

At the site, adding blocks with Tomate's camera recording nearby, the lights start flickering.

> **Foolish:** "something like that for now.. hmm, i dont think i made enough—" ... "uh- tomate?"

Lightning strikes, again and again. Tomate laughs.

> **Foolish:** "That's not funny, we could be in danger!" ... "Oh no, did i mess with the wrong blocks?! was i not supposed to mess with those blocks..!? I thought i was trying to help!! Did I do the wrong thing?—"

He freaks out, then listens — something whispering, demonic-sounding.

> **Foolish:** "Is it saying something?… Hello? Is that you, Fata Error?"

**Dark Cucurucho** speaks — Cucurucho's voice, distorted.

> **Dark Cucurucho:** "Thank you my child. For you have put the first block, I am now able to prove that you are the one who wishes to build my temple."
> **Foolish:** "No way…" ... "Well then… Let's get started."
> **Dark Cucurucho:** "You are the one who wishes to build my temple?"
> **Foolish:** "Yeah, yeah, thats me."
> **Dark Cucurucho:** "Good. Build my temple with the required specifications and I will be freed."
> **Foolish:** "Perfect.. Just what i was looking to do! You know— Wow! I did not think you were the one I would be working with.."
> **Dark Cucurucho:** "Before we begin. Are you loyal to me?"
> **Foolish:** "Yeah.. Let's get started."
> **Dark Cucurucho:** "Are you willing to die for me?"
> **Foolish:** "Sure.. Not my first time."
> **Dark Cucurucho:** "I am unable to trust human mortals. You soul will be bound to me until my temple is complete."
> **Foolish:** "Oh.. Okay.."
> **Dark Cucurucho:** "Are you ready to begin?"
> **Foolish:** "Hm.. Yeah, yes dark lord— Let's start." *(nervous joke; doesn't land — evil entities apparently lack much of a sense of humor)*
> **Dark Cucurucho:** "Repeat these words. Yes Dark Cucurucho I am ready to be your servant and build your temple. May my soul the yearn to you that the job will be comple."
> **Foolish:** "Uh eh- Why is it so many words, okay..— Yes dark cucurucho I will be your servant and build your temple. And may my soul proof as guarantee that the mission will get done—? Is that good enough, that was a very long stretch of-"

Another strike, closer than before.

> **Dark Cucurucho:** "it will be weeks before your friends ever see you again. But for you, it will be immediate. Do not fret, I have a replacement for you in your absence!"
> **Foolish:** "Huh?"

Lightning strikes again, closer still.

> **Dark Cucurucho:** "Good. Time to go."

*(Scene ends here, per author — a deliberate cliffhanger.)*
