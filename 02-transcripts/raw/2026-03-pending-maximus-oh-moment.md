## Notes

Same mixed-source convention as prior entries. Context, per author: Juan,
trusting Maximus at the time, told him that Foolish planned to join the
Federation as an undercover double-agent. Foolish had told Juan about
this plan first (before anyone else — see [EV-0030](../../01-canon-timeline/ev-0030-federation-recruitment-arc.md));
Juan had called him a "traitor," mostly playfully, not truly believing it
once Foolish explained further. Maximus and other past-island inhabitants
harbor much deeper distrust of the Federation — and of Foolish
specifically, since Foolish did something similar on QSMP1: went
undercover, ended up with "blood on his hands," and was even named the
Federation's "employee of the month" — a history Foolish himself compared
to Juan's own "office simulator" habit. Juan learned all of this and
still trusted Foolish, reasoning that a real traitor wouldn't have told
him in the first place.

## Transcript

> **Juan:** "—El sabe que nos beneficia. Y antes de aceptarlo hablo con nosotros. El no es alguien en quien desconfiar No es la persona mas lista, mucho menos lamas guapa, ni siquiera el mas gracioso. Es.. completamente irritante, estupido, burdo. Una completa porquería con vida. Pero- no es un falso, ni un traidor."
> **Maximus:** "¿Tu crees que no es un traidor?—"
> **Juan:** "absolutamente."
> **Maximus:** "Tu crees que no nos tracionar, tu crees que no le habrá contado ya a la federación—"
> **Juan:** "Esa chingadera duras penas, no se caga encima! Obviamente no nos va a traicionar!"
> **Maximus:** "Y si descubres que si?.. Y si descubres que es el el que le esta dando información a la federación?"
> **Juan:** "Tú crees que yo podria traicionar al norte? En algun momento, Maximus?—"
> **Maximus:** "Yo creo, yo creo que a ti te gusta Foolish. Y de ahi, ese odio irracional que tienes hacia el."
> **Juan:** "Es la mamada mas grande que has dicho."
> **Maximus:** "O-O los mad logico que he dijo."

## Juan's reflection, afterward

> **Maximus's line, replaying in his head:** "Yo creo, yo creo que a ti te gusta Foolish. Y de ahi, ese odio irracional que tienes hacia el."

It made more sense than he'd like to admit aloud, or even to himself.
Foolish pushed his buttons more than anyone else, but even so he
constantly, consciously or not, sought out his company. He repeated his
own earlier words in his mind, almost like a mantra:

> **Juan, replaying his own words:** "No es la persona mas lista, mucho menos lamas guapa, ni siquiera el mas gracioso. Es.. completamente irritante, estupido, burdo. Una completa porquería con vida."

Obviously he didn't truly hate him — it was just their thing, to
constantly argue and belittle each other. He wondered, for a moment, if
Foolish truly believed any of the things he said. Because he was only
just starting to realize — unas mamadas.
