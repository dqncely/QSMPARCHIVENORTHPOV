## Transcript

Roier and Foolish were talking, Foolish recognized him from his traumatic
time on an island. Ironically, this cruise was eerily similar to the one
he'd been taking before when he got stuck on a island (QSMP1). He was
happy to see a familiar face, especially because he remembered holding
paternal affection towards Roier on that island.

(For context, Roier seems to have quite a bit of memory loss from the
last island.)

Roier looked around and saw a familiar face, a good friend of his, maybe
from childhood, he couldn't remember the last time they'd spoken. There
was a large blank chunk in his life recently. Juan looked over and
smiled, waving happily. Roier dragged Foolish toward him, saying:

> **R:** Este es mi amigo, Juan.

Juan inspected the man(?). He looked a bit strange, but there were a
couple other weirdos on the ship as well.

> **J:** …Hola

> **F:** Hola, Hola, Juan! Yo soy Foolish.

Juan could immediately tell Spanish wasn't his first language, similarly
to most on the ship. Supposedly, there would be all sorts of different
languages spoken. Juan, for the most part, stuck with others who spoke
Spanish.

> **J:** Hola Foolish..

> **F:** Yo estoy… Eh, se muchas palabras en Español.

He explained, slowly figuring his words out as he spoke. Juan smiled,
easy. (They don't yet have their "rivalry", mostly just meeting each
other right now.)

> **J:** Oh, nice.. Yo se muchas palabras en Inglés!

> **F:** Oh! hohoho-

> **J:** -yess

> **F:** Ah-

Juan looked around at the others standing close by, and admitted:

> **J:** Mentí.

Foolish didn't notice, someone nearby asked him if he knew any bad words
in Spanish.

> **F:** Eh- Como? Oh! Ah.. Pendejo.., y mierda-

Juan spit out the drink he'd been sipping on, giggling. The guy asked him
to say "the best ones", Juan added:

> **J:** Ey, pero te lo esta diciendo a ti, eh?

The guy replied:

> No, ya se wey me esta insultando pero lo acepto, lo acepto.

Juan laughed. Foolish was still thinking:

> **F:** Uh.. What was- C-culo tu madre?

> **J:** No, culero.

Juan corrected, though not entirely sure if that was what the other was
referring to. Foolish echoed his words:

> **F:** Oh, culero, culero!

> Que le estas enseñando, Juan?

The guy asked, bumping his shoulder.

> **F:** Chinga tu madre..?

Foolish said, almost as a question. Juan laughed again, just as Roier
interjected, pulling him away to introduce him to someone else. *Que tipo
interesante..*

Also worth noting: Juan, in this moment in his life, has just come back
from literal prison — he'd gone to jail for something very serious and
gotten out years later with no money or place to go, so he decided to get
on a special cruise with people of all sorts of languages and walks of
life. When he first gets on he's surprised to see many different and
strange-looking people, but he's always been drawn to weirdos.

As for Foolish, he was invited since he was on the first island and
survived purgatory — he has history with the island and he's one of the
only ones who actually remembers what happened, although this time he's
making a promise to himself to not get attached to anyone and just "enjoy
the island", and maybe take them down.
