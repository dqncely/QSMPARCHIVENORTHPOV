## Content

**Federation gives Fariis a task**, per author: kill Foolish today, in exchange for his helmet and sword back. Foolish had already overheard Fariis discussing working with the Federation (with someone named Pepino).

**Cucurucho leaves Juan's teddy bear as a sign** for Aldo, meant to pressure him toward completing his task. The sign reads: "See you Soon."

> Reports indicate that the object was left by Cucurucho, which has sparked concern due to the tone of the message. The situation is being interpreted as a possible attempt at psychological pressure on Q!Aldo, amid the current context where the Federation has imposed certain orders on him to fulfill.

Foolish, along with Graf, Mike, and/or Tina, is among the first to find the teddy — he mentions Juan has one too but dismisses the idea it's specifically his. Aldo later finds it, recognizes it's from Cucurucho, understands it's connected to his "mission" without fully grasping how.

**Cucurucho meets Aldo** to discuss the kill-task, first showing him a video confirming Juan's kidnapping. He gives Aldo the "Penetrating Strike" enchantment specifically to help him kill Ash, with a deadline of Monday.

> Based on what was observed, Q!Aldo asked that no harm be done to Q!Juan and demanded to see him, to which Cucurucho responded in an unsettling way: he showed him a teddy bear saying "here he is," then withdrew without giving further explanations.

**Fariis, spying on the Aldo/Cucurucho/Federation conversation, is caught and killed** by Cucurucho on the spot, his belongings confiscated. When Fariis later returns (after the conversation ended) to ask about his things, Aldo tells him the Federation took them, and denies working for the Federation when asked directly what he'd overheard — though Fariis remains unconvinced on both counts.

**Aldo turns himself in** rather than complete the kill-task alone, which he recognizes as effectively a suicide mission. Tina is present.

> **Aldo:** "will be back, okay? I will find a way. Maybe this is a rare opportunity for us to get closer to the federation.. Like alondra is doing, okay?"
> **Tina:** "You'll come back?"
> **Aldo:** "You are now the Generalisima! Okay? You have my rank—"
> **Tina:** "No, no—"
> **Aldo:** "—Make me proud, girl!"

**Tina tells Foolish.** He notices her red-rimmed eyes immediately.

> **Tina:** "hey.."
> **Foolish:** "Hello! What's up?" *(hoping to cheer her up — did Aldo fire her again?)*
> **Tina:** "They took him.."
> **Foolish:** ".. Who took what?"
> **Tina:** "The federation… They took him."
> **Foolish:** "Who..?"

*(She walks into Aldo's room, looking at a photo of him and Tina — "the generalísimos" — her voice smaller than Foolish has ever heard it.)*

> **Tina:** "..Aldo."
> **Foolish:** "..Wait, why?"
> **Tina:** "They gave him an impossible task."

*(She walks out; Foolish follows.)*

> **Foolish:** "Wait.. So it's- It's off the table? The- The task?"
> **Tina:** ".. Yes."
> **Foolish:** "-Well I thought we had one more day! Oh no.. Oh no, so you're telling me right now.. Our general, and our second in command— plus deluxe is gone?"
> **Tina:** "Mhm.."
> **Foolish:** "Oh my Goodness.. Oh my Goodness.. Did- Did they saying anything? Like- Like 'you're gonna be in prison- or you're gonna be jailed for a week?'"
> **Tina:** "I think we're gonna get Juan back.."

*(Foolish feels relief, immediately replaced by guilt.)*

> **Foolish:** "Oh.. He went to do a trade?!"
> **Tina:** "That's cool, right.. Yeah, yeah.. He um- He turned himself in."
> **Foolish:** "Wait, he turned himself in?"
> **Tina:** "Yeah, isn't that amazing..hah-"
> **Foolish:** "No but- We still had a plan! We still had a day, no?!"
> **Tina:** "Isn't that so cool? That he would do that, our general?"

*(Foolish, unsure what to do, tries to hug her.)*

> **Foolish:** "Awe.."
> **Tina:** "Ah- No no!"
> **Foolish:** "Oh, sorry.. hah.. I know I'm a little creepy.."

*(They laugh briefly; Tina sighs.)*

> **Foolish:** "Okay.. um!.. So he went to trade himself- but what? W-Well, I thought we had a plan? We were talking about it today"
> **Tina:** "They didn't like it, Foolish. They didn't like the plan. They didn't like that.. It was you that killed him, you know? It had to be him.."
> **Foolish:** "..So they didn't accept it."
> **Tina:** "No, they got mad saying that- you know, we were trying to make cucurucho look stupid."
> **Foolish:** "..Okay.. So what if he is?"
> **Tina:** "Well- Shh."
> **Foolish:** "Shh.."
> **Tina** *(whispering)*: "They hear, and see everything, okay? okay?"
> **Foolish** *(whispering, walking toward Tina's room)*: "Hm.. yeah that had been the case."

*(Author's note: Foolish ends up calling an emergency conference even though it's just the two of them — something Juan would've done — and Tina repeats everything Aldo used to say.
