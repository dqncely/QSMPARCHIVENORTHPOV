## Transcript

Foolish walks into Juan's office without knocking, surprised to find
Ishan already there with Juan — looks like a meeting in progress.

> **Foolish:** "oh. Oh, Hello!"
> **Juan:** "Hello, foolish… We're talking about you."
> **Ishan:** "Hello."
> **Foolish:** "Oh, probably great things."
> **Juan:** "Yeah, absolutely. We're talking a lot of good things about you!"
> **Foolish:** "Oh, do you know something amazing happened today?"
> **Juan:** "Yeah. Yeah. Yeah. You have a new a new rank, right? You are now the second in command plus. Congrats."
> **Foolish:** "New rank! Second in command plus! Thank you. Thank you. I've worked really hard."
> **Ishan:** "So you are above Tina now?"
> **Foolish:** "No. No. No,"
> **Ishan:** "not yet..?"
> **Foolish:** "I'm above a frog now."

Juan looks pleased.

> **Juan:** "Yeah!! That's a very good work!" *(laughter)* "Uh, foolish. What is this about you have an office?"

A menacing smirk crosses Foolish's face.

> **Foolish:** "Oh, well, here we could talk there if you want. I just figured since um now that I have a plus, I should probably have an office. So, I got to work."
> **Juan:** "Yeah, let's go to your office."
> **Foolish:** "Yeah, let's go to my office. Let's go to my office. Um,"

They head downstairs to Foolish's room. There's a new elevator to a room below that definitely wasn't there before.

> **Foolish:** "Oh, and yeah, then just an elevator right here. Oh, okay."

Juan looks around for about 30 seconds — already irritated. It's an exact copy of Juan's office, down to the pictures.

> **Juan:** "What the Fuck, stupid?"
> **Foolish:** "Welcome to my office!"
> **Juan:** "No, your office is exactly the same—"

Juan opens the side door — in his own office it leads to his room; in Foolish's it leads to nothing but open air outside the mansion. He nearly falls several stories.

> **Juan:** "What the-This not have sense! What is this?"

He steps closer to Foolish and notices what he's wearing.

> **Foolish:** "Well, you know—" *(grinning wide enough to show fangs)*
> **Juan:** "What are- Your clothes are stupid!"

*(He usually avoids looking at that part of Foolish, who almost never wears a shirt, but today it's a green short-sleeve dress shirt with a tie and tucked brown pants — strikingly similar to Juan's own yellow short-sleeve dress shirt. He realizes: it IS his shirt, now straining at the seams. He looks away.)*

> **Foolish:** "Wait, how are my clothes stupid? I feel like they look good—"
> **Juan:** "It's my clothes! What are you doing?!"
> **Foolish:** "So, if you think your these clothes are stupid and this room is stupid, what would that make you in your place?"
> **Juan:** "No, I'm cool because my style! But this you're Fucking— What the Fuck?"
> **Foolish:** "Can you close the door? It's windy."

Juan slams the door shut — carefully avoiding the edge this time — and breaks a glass pane in the process. Foolish gasps dramatically.

> **Juan:** "Yeah, don't worry. I can do anything that I want." *(walks around, shoving things)*
> **Foolish:** "Hey! Hey, this is my office!"
> **Juan:** *(mimicking)* "neh neh neh is my office!" "You need a new style. It's exactly that my room and it's more bigger! You suck, You're stupid and I hate you!"

Foolish starts raising his voice too.

> **Foolish:** "Yeah, it is bigger! I made it better! I made it bigger and bett-"
> **Juan:** "Put this glass off. Put this glass off."
> **Foolish:** "Yeah, this office. This is me oficina ahora!"
> **Juan:** "Yeah but this is- What-"

*(Juan short-circuits, torn between finding English words and staring at Foolish's shirt stretched over him — he just yells.)*

> **Juan:** *(screaming)* "NO, I don't like it! No, No!"
> **Foolish:** "Mi alfeombra es mas grande que-"

They talk over each other; despite his smaller frame, Juan's voice carries.

> **Juan:** "Okay, I'm gonna kill you, I'm fucking gonna kill you!"
> **Foolish:** "Oh, you're gonna kill me? Really?"
> **Juan:** "Yeah, when you're sleeping,"
> **Foolish:** "Oh, wow!"
> **Juan:** "I'm gonna enter in your room and I'm fucking gonna kill you!"
> **Foolish:** "Wow, that is really mean. On my big promotion day."
> **Juan:** "Yeah- I don't give a fuck your promotion! Yo- You know- You know what? Y-You are the second in command plus. B- But, but for the stupids. Yeah, yeah"

Foolish laughs.

> **Foolish:** "But I still have a plus. That's right. I still have a plus!"

Juan walks over to a framed picture he doesn't have in his own room.

> **Juan:** "What is this?"
> **Foolish:** "Huh? Oh, that's just a picture of me and you!"
> **Juan:** "Oh, that's cool. hehe."

He tries the door that in his own room leads to the hallway — another steep drop.

> **Juan:** "What.. What the-? This not have sense! I really hate this room—" *(yelling, as Foolish keels over laughing)* "—It's Horrible!"
> **Foolish:** "what? I just thought- I thought you'd like it, I thought it'd feel cozy for you."
> **Juan:** "No! It's more bigger, It's so scare! And I don't like your clothes! And-"

Foolish walks over, standing as close to "shoulder to shoulder" as their height difference allows.

> **Foolish:** "Look, we look great! It looks like we're really working together."
> **Ishan:** "Yeah, you look like twins now!"
> **Foolish:** "Wait, I think we bought our pants from the same place" *(said innocently — he'd had to buy new pants since Juan's wouldn't fit; he'd barely squeezed into the shirt)*
> **Juan:** "Yeah, yeah, because y-you are- you're.. Oh my God."

He sits down, unable to keep facing Foolish. Ishan sits next to him; Juan tries to resume the earlier meeting.

> **Juan:** "Well, Cucurucho. What's about Cucurucho? Yeah. Yeah…"

He realizes he's sitting in the wrong seat.

> **Juan:** "I don't want to be here!" *(gets up, walks to where Foolish is sitting)* "Sit there, sit there!"
> **Foolish:** "No, this is my office!"
> **Juan:** "No No—"
> **Foolish:** "Mi oficina!"
> **Juan:** "I'm second in command plus plus! When you sit in my office, you sit —here!" *(points at the chair he'd just left, next to Ishan)*
> **Foolish:** "Si. Pero. Esta mi oficina aquí!"
> **Juan:** "Okay, let's go to my office."
