## Content

**Immediately after their last talk** (author summary): nothing seemed
out of the ordinary yet. Foolish went back to hunting the entity alone,
theorizing that being solo might draw it out. He camped with Mike, who
got (or pretended to get) possessed — a fun bit. Back at spawn, they met
Molly, Tina, Alondrissa, and Senpai Spider, whose "girls' night" (trying
to summon a ghost) had been ruined by the entity chasing them down.
Foolish, baffled that the thing went after everyone but him, got pulled
into yet another adventure — distracted enough that nobody puts together
what happened until the next morning. Meanwhile Aldo was already asking
where Juan had gone; Tina hadn't seen him since the meeting. They start
connecting the dots.

Foolish notices Juan's absence almost immediately, at first assuming
laziness — Juan almost never leaves his office, let alone the mansion.
After more time passes it starts to feel wrong. *Maybe we're just
missing each other,* he tells himself.

He keeps monster-hunting with other members, bringing up Juan's absence
a few times without alarm. He visits Juan's office once meaning to prank
it, then remembers it's Juan's day for the prank rotation — instead puts
up a photo of the two of them smiling, saying "Juan would love this."
Later he starts telling people Juan's just being lazy, hasn't logged in
at all that day.

> **Foolish:** "Damn it, where is Juan?? He's been gone for so long [...] the last thing he said to me, the last thing I heard him say was 'You're a really good second-in-command and they need you'"

When Pac and Mike suggest maybe Juan got sick of working, Foolish agrees out loud — but doesn't believe it. *Abandon work? Never.*

**Later, the North finds out.** Aldo calls a meeting with whoever he can find: Alondra, Roier, Foolish.

> **Aldo:** "Estábamos arriba cucurucho y yo en el Castillo. hablando sobre el cometido y al parecer farris escucho.. No se que tanto entendió pero peo lo alcance a noquear mientras estaba invisible y el policía lo remato. Le quitaron todas sus cosas."
> **Roier:** "No mames cabron."
> **Aldo:** "Si. cucurucho sabe que mate a farris, no se si farris sepa honestamente."
> **Roier:** "y te va a perdonar? o.. o mas o meos?"
> **Aldo:** "..amigos hay una noticia muy cabrona que tengo que darles, okay?"
> **Roier:** "dime, dime."
> **Alondra:** "paso algo malo?"
> **Aldo:** "secuestraron a Juan. Cucurucho tiene a Juan secuestrado."
> **Roier:** "Como?"
> **Alondra:** "En serio?"
> **Foolish:** "…What?"
> **Roier:** "por eso es que no esta en el puto cuarto el pendejo! Siempre esta en el jueves de lechita!"
> **Alondra:** "Ayer yo tampoco lo vi."
> **Aldo:** "Me dio fecha limite de.. De hasta el Lunes para matar a todos Del regime si no—"
> **Roier:** "A todos?!"
> **Aldo:** "Si."
> **Alondra:** "Es un hijo de puta!"

Foolish, pacing, the words finally settling:

> **Foolish:** "They kidnapped Juan!!"
> **Aldo:** "Con razon ayer habia un perro en la casa.. Y el perro me dijo que Juan no iba volver."
> **Roier:** "No mames, de dijo eso?—"

Roier and Alondra keep talking — noise to Foolish, who can't focus enough to follow.

> **Foolish:** "He's probably so scared right now.."

Voices drop when Fariis is heard nearby. Foolish remembers his earlier conversation with the man — he'd meant to keep it private, but now it isn't just about weaponry.

> **Foolish:** "Okay, wait- I should explain then what i know about farris since he was spying. Basically, the reason he killed me was because the federation told him that if he killed me, they would give him like a new— netherite sword.— cause he like lost his somehow. So, it feels like the federation is trying to cause a war. They're having us try to kill them, and they're sending hits out on us for the regime to do."

Not his usual role — he'd always been the comic relief in moments like this — but not now. He can't stand being in the room any longer as the others' words blur into noise; a chat message ("anyone have a very very far away waystone?") gives him an excuse to leave, to go help Mike instead.

**A bit later, Foolish and Aldo talk alone at the castle:**

> **Aldo:** "What will we do about Juan's situation?"
> **Foolish:** "I think our only option left is to kill the members of the regime, because I don't think we can get him out of where he is."
> **Aldo:** "But what if this provokes a big war?(...)"
> **Foolish:** "Well, so be it. We have our backs against the wall right now."

*(Foolish, despite occasionally joking that his "second in command deluxe" role is temporary, seems willing to do whatever it takes.)*

Quotes from Foolish at the kelp dragon, alone:

> **Foolish:** "wait, you're right, he believed in me. He knows it's his time, he's already reached the end. They kidnapped him and now he's dead, we did everything we could. It's like he wanted me to have his job."

> **Foolish:** "i wonder how juan got kidnapped... it's a little funny but also estoy triste.."

*(He keeps joking about these things, but doesn't seem to genuinely believe Juan won't return — and seems to want to say the jokes to Juan's face specifically, not just to himself.)*
