## Transcript

Foolish has just invited Juan monster hunting; Juan agrees faster than
he thinks about it, then remembers he has work to finish first.

> **Juan:** "—But I need to finish something to Aldo."
> **Foolish:** "Ohh.. Okay,"
> **Juan:** "You're gonna see in a minute, don't worry."

Foolish starts walking out, repeating himself.

> **Foolish:** "Okay, Okay."

Juan follows.

> **Juan:** "I'm free like in.. 10, 20 minutes?" *(as Foolish closes the office door in his face — Juan just opens it and keeps following)* "We gonna have a meeting, okay?"
> **Foolish:** "Mmm.."

*(He heads down the stairs, Juan at his heels, starting to understand.)*

> **Juan:** "Not here— oh, you're mad? because i'm working?"

*(Foolish laughs like that's the stupidest thing he's heard all day; Juan laughs too, finding the whole thing ridiculous.)*

> **Foolish:** "I just- I just think you're stupid." *(scrambling for something normal to say)*
> **Juan:** "Hehe.. What?! Why?"
> **Foolish:** "I don't know cause- cause o-of those glasses." *(stuttering — why is he stuttering?)*
> **Juan:** "So rude!" *(giggling, his accent making the word sound made up — Foolish likes that)*
> **Foolish:** "You just like- i don't know I look at you and you're just stupid."
> **Juan:** "I look like the Captain America." *(wearing a military-style uniform outfit that day — for PvP training or a later meeting)*
> **Foolish:** "No, you do not look like Captain America."
> **Juan:** "Yeah, I'm like.. Steve Rodgers."
> **Foolish:** "Get back to work."

They finally reach the forest.

> **Foolish:** "Um.. Wait, Tubbo just told me to go to my dragon."
> **Juan:** "Oh, say Tubbo that.. Fuck him."
> **Foolish:** "Oh, I can't do that."
> **Juan:** "Oh, because he's your boyfriend? Ah?"
> **Foolish:** "No it's just—"
> **Juan:** "Ah? Because it's your new boyfriend, after Vegetta you need a be new boyfriend because you feel so alonee??"
> **Foolish:** "You know what? You should go talk to robleis again."
> **Juan:** "Oh, Fuck you."
> **Foolish:** "Yeah, speaking of boyfriends."
> **Juan:** "No I don't want talk to him never. He's not my boyfriend!—"
> **Foolish:** "Well-"
> **Juan:** "—Cucurucho was my boyfriend."
> **Foolish:** "No, no, he never was."
> **Juan:** "Yeah, but i think this."
> **Foolish:** "Hehe, you're crazy."
> **Juan:** "Oh, look! The monster!" *(points at a random sheep)*
