- **Link:** Not provided

## Transcript — scouting the park

Juan and Foolish go to Cucurucho Land to plan Graf's second date. Both loudly insist the park is terrible while repeatedly choosing to try its attractions.

> **Juan:** "Oh my God. Look.. This place sucks!"
>
> **Foolish:** "Uh.. No, it's horrible.. His face is everywhere."
>
> **Juan:** "Oh, I hate this place.. Is so horrible, I hate it."
>
> **Foolish:** "Wait- this could be…the date tomorrow! You go here and then when you're on the Ferris wheel, that's where you tell him!"
>
> **Juan:** "Yeass! Okay.. Yeah..! It's a very good idea."

They inspect the park's muscular Cucurucho shopkeeper, rides, statues, games, and carousel. Juan calls the shopkeeper hot as a joke; Foolish replies, "Lowkey." They ride the roller coaster and teacups while loudly claiming to hate them, play whack-a-Cucurucho, and ride the carousel.

> **Juan, on the roller coaster:** "I FUCKING HATE IT! ITS SO FUCKING BORING!! THIS PLACE SUCKS A LOT!.. ITS A TOTAL SHIT!"
>
> **Foolish:** "ITS AWESOME!"
>
> **Juan:** "FUCK, NO! ITS NOT AWESOME!"
>
> **Juan, on the carousel:** "YEAHH! Cariño? Es.. Soy cariño?"
>
> **Foolish:** "Is that.. Cowboy?? What's cariño?"
>
> **Juan:** "I don't know!!?"

They settle on the park as the second-date location. Foolish promises to watch and keep a Juan disguise ready as backup. Before leaving, they decide to ride the roller coaster twice more "to make sure" it is bad.

> **Juan:** "We need to prepare so good everything tomorrow. I don't want that Graf get mad. Or- Feel bad."
>
> **Foolish:** "You could like just be bad at this. Maybe it will make him not wanna be with you."

## Transcript — Graf arrives

While Juan tries the archery game, Graf appears.

> **Graf:** "Evening. Foolish, I haven't seen you in a long time."
>
> **Foolish:** "Oh, dude. I know- I've just been.. so busy camping. It's great to see you for the first time today and in forever!"
>
> **Juan:** "Yeah, it's the first time that Foolish talk with you and see you…"
>
> **Graf:** "Haven't seen him even once today."
>
> **Juan:** "Ey, I have very good news for you Graf, tomorrow the- Uh, forget it! Is gonna be a surprise for tomorrow."

Juan calls for a group hug rather than hugging Graf alone, then leaves. Foolish and Graf explore the Nether together. Foolish finds Graf helpful, thoughtful, multilingual, and sincere.
