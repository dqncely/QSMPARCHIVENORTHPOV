## Notes

Same mixed-source convention as prior entries. Per author: for the day
or two before his release, Juan had been hearing music blasting twice —
a K-pop group Aldo listens to. Context: on Aldo's first day in
captivity, he'd demanded guards be "stuck there with him" and played
loud music to make the point. Aldo was being forced to watch Juan
tortured, which is why he was kept in the same facility after turning
himself in — and the Federation has been slowly brainwashing him to
believe the North isn't looking for him and doesn't care he's gone. Juan
does not yet know any of this when he's released — he learns it from
Tina shortly after, at which point the music finally makes sense in
hindsight.
