# The Dark Cucurucho sighting

- **Channel/streamer:** Not provided
- **Date:** Unknown — after Juan and Foolish's reunion, before the temple reveal.
- **Link:** Not provided
- **Language:** Mixed English/Spanish
- **Coverage:** Full, as provided.

## Notes

Same mixed-source convention as prior entries. This is the first time
Juan encounters Dark Cucurucho directly (unknowingly) since Foolish's
soul pact in [EV-0055](../../01-canon-timeline/ev-0055-dark-cucurucho-ritual.md) —
Juan has no context for what he's seeing yet.

## Transcript

Juan and Foolish are talking — Juan complaining about Foolish building a "death star" with Tubbo (a Regime member) — when a figure appears in the water, unnatural.

> **Juan:** "Uh- Foolish!? Foolish que es eso??" *(switching to Spanish, caught off guard)*
> **Foolish:** *(freezes)* "Uh- Are you seeing that?"
> **Juan:** "Foolish, Que verga es eso, mamon??!"
> **Foolish:** "um.."
> **Juan:** "FOOLISH? QUE ES ESO"
> **Foolish:** "UH-"

*(Juan pulls out a boat.)*

> **Juan:** "Foolish hay que irnos, YA, SUBE, SUBE"
> **Foolish:** "Okay, listen- I-"
> **Juan:** "SUBE, PUTA MADRE, SUBE!"
> **Foolish:** "OKAY, HES- HES SOMEONE, WE'VE SEEN HIM BEFORE-"

*(Juan teleports them to his room at the North. Tubbo, left behind, asks what's wrong just as they vanish.)*

> **Juan:** "OH MY GOD, FUCK YOU TUBBO!" *(then)* "Okay.. What the fuck? OH MY FUCKING GOD, MY HOUSE!"

*(Foolish is struck by lightning.)*

> **Juan:** "It's not safe here, its not-"
> **Foolish:** "Okay, Don't worry, dont worry-"

*(Juan dumps water on Foolish, who's on fire.)*

> **Foolish:** "Juan- You have to understand, did you see that guy?!"
> **Juan:** "Yeah, i see!"

## Content — the explanation, and Foolish's disappearance

Foolish explains there's a "dark cucurucho," someone he met once before
on the first island, an enemy of the Federation — or maybe of Cucurucho,
or maybe of everyone. Juan decides to call him "good cucurucho" and
(per his own phrasing, kept as spoken) "blackface cucurucho" — his own
attempt to describe the dark coloring, not further explained. Tubbo and
Graf come to discuss what happened and who Dark Cucurucho is. At some
point, while everyone's deciding to sleep it off, Foolish vanishes — and
it doesn't turn day.

> **Juan:** "Where is Foolish? Guys, where is Foolish?!"
> **Tina:** "Wait.. Where is Foolish?"
> **Juan:** "Oh my god.."

*(He asks in chat; Foolish replies that he was teleported.)*

> **Juan:** "Okay, Tubbo we have a mission, we need to rescue Foolish!"
> **Tubbo:** "Rescue from what?"
> **Juan:** "For I dont know-!? Far away in the wildness.."

*(He reads Foolish's next message aloud.)*

> **Juan:** "Okay, we need to found Foolish."
> **Tubbo:** "Uh.. Juan, this is not good"
> **Juan:** "He can die.." *(worried)*
> **Tubbo:** "He can't die, he's a totem of undying, bestie. Um.."

*(Juan doesn't understand why everyone uses that excuse to minimize things when it comes to Foolish — like it doesn't still hurt.)*

> **Juan:** "But.. He's in danger."
> **Tubbo:** "Uh.. Well, he won't where he is so we can't do anything right now, I'm sure he's fine."

*(Juan climbs a nearby plant decoration, agitated.)*

> **Juan:** "H-He's not fine- This thing was so huge.. Like, this type of huge-"
> **Tubbo:** "Huge? Like that tall?"
> **Juan:** "Yeah like.. Oh, I'm black cucurucho and i'm good but i look so bad because im dark"
> **Tubbo:** "no, he's kind of scary, but lovable, thats kinds his thing."
> **Juan:** "Maybe i can be with.. the dark cucurucho, because the-"
> **Tubbo:** "Yeah, maybe he can show you a passionate touch-"
> **Juan:** "yeah, because the real cucurucho is not a good man, you know? He's a manchild."

Automated-looking Federation-style messages appear in chat, red instead of the usual color:

> **Chat:** "-Everyone say. Thank you FoolishG!-"
> **Juan:** "Uh- Tubbo?"
> **Tubbo:** "Yeah?"
> **Juan:** "you see this?"
> **Tubbo:** "yeah, we're just showing him some appreciation, probably."
> **Juan:** "He's in danger."
> **Tubbo:** "no, no we're thanking him for showing the light or something!"

*(Juan, already disliking Tubbo, types in chat: "FOOLISH IS IN DANGER GUYS")*

> **Tubbo:** "how do you know he's in danger?"
> **Juan:** "I don't know he was kidnapped.."

*(Another message: "-please disregard any Messages on your screen. We are experiencing a glitch within our announcements system.-")*

> **Juan:** "What the fuck-? They are fighting? Oh, thats great! I wanna see the fight!"

*(Tubbo reads more of the chat; Juan rolls his eyes.)*

> **Tubbo:** "Classic. oh geez, isn't this a stressful situation to find yourself in?"
> **Juan:** "…oh my God, that's great, WE HAVE A HERO! We are saved!"
> **Tubbo:** "yeah, dark cucurucho is kind of a hero"
> **Juan:** "yeah, that's great right? So we're saved."
> **Tubbo:** "Well.. Were we in danger Before..?"
> **Juan:** "yeah, obviously the federation… I was in danger."
> **Tubbo:** "what did they do?"
> **Juan:** "I was kidnapped!!"
> **Tubbo:** "but you came back"
> **Juan:** "oh my God, Tubbo! I was kidnapped- like ten days! and- and then.."
> **Tubbo:** "Wait you were gone for 10 days!??"
> **Juan:** "Yeah, was so long! was a fucking nightmare"

*(Juan, definitively, does not like this guy.)*

## Content — Jueves de Lechita and Foolish's return message

Juan meets up with Ishan and Senpai, both dressed for that week's Jueves
de Lechita theme (dress as a North member): Juan went as Foolish, Ishan
as Tina, Senpai as Vegetta, Foolish as Roier, and Tina as Juan. Foolish
finally messages in chat; Juan switches to private messages.

> **Juan:** "ARE U OK???" ... "WHERE ARE U? WANT THAT I GO FOR U??"
> **Foolish:** "MEET AT DRAGON"

Juan wraps up his conversation with Graf and heads straight there.
