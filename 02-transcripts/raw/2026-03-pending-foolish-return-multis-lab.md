## Notes

Same mixed-source convention as prior entries. Foolish regained
consciousness a day before this scene, in a random patch of grass near a
church, with no memory of how he got there — only his mission. He spent
that day working on the temple before finally messaging Juan.

*(Juan and Foolish laugh, explaining the joke to Multi, who remembers another question.)*
