## Content — how leave works on the island (worldbuilding, not a scene)

Per author: island members are allowed a few days' leave for special
occasions — weddings, important events, and (for more popular members
like Katie B.) even vacations — but are required to come back, whether
willingly or by force. Sometimes they don't remember how they got back.
This exists in-universe to avoid raising suspicion from real-world family
or friends members might have outside the island. [MY INTERPRETATION —
worldbuilding note, not a specific dated event]

## Content — before the week away (brief scenes, summarized)

Juan and Foolish, dressed as the "smiling friends," try to cheer Katie B.
up about her (fake-dead, from an earlier bit) husband Tomate. They
travel back to the site of Juan's captivity with Ash, searching
unsuccessfully for Tomate — a real exposure-therapy moment for Juan,
walking around the place he was held, with Foolish's encouragement
getting him through the fear. Separately, Juan's painting commission
business is underway — he paints Vegetta and Ash first, then Foolish
commissions his own portrait for one netherite ingot (haggled down by
Ash on Juan's behalf).
