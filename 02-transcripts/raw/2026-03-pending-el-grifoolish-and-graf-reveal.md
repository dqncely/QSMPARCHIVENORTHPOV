# "El Grifoolish" and the Graf reveal date

- **Channel/streamer:** Not provided
- **Date:** Unknown — a day or two after both Juan and Foolish returned from their week away ([EV-0065](../../01-canon-timeline/ev-0065-hiatus-week-away-and-return.md)).
- **Link:** Not provided
- **Language:** Mixed English/Spanish/Polish
- **Coverage:** Full, as provided. Part 1 of a "Graf dates trilogy," per author.

## Notes

Same mixed-source convention as prior entries. Juan's shop is named "Mi
Arte" — a bilingual pun (Spanish for "my art," but sounds like "miarte,"
Mexican slang for wetting yourself). Graf had been messaging Juan
strange, seemingly unrelated questions (favorite food) in the lead-up to
this — in hindsight, obviously test-runs for a date, missed entirely by
Juan at the time.
