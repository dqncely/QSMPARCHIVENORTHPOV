# The heart saga, part 1 — Doctor Atomico

- **Channel/streamer:** Not provided
- **Date:** Unknown — pending confirmation. Positioned by the author "sometime after" the office wars/therapy period, when Juan and Foolish are "less full on rivals, more friends who bicker." Author flags this as important lore for later plot points.
- **Link:** Not provided

- **Coverage:** Part 1 of a longer saga, as provided. Author summarized the remainder (see Notes) rather than transcribing it verbatim; that summary is marked accordingly, not treated as a direct quote.

## Notes

Same mixed-source convention as prior entries. Juan role-plays as
"Doctor Atomico," a self-proclaimed scientist, in his own bit — using
Foolish's room as his "laboratory." Participants: Juan ("Doctor
Atomico"), [Abueloier](../../03-character-sheets/abueloier.md) (reportedly
near death), Nexe, and Foolish.

**Author's summary of how the saga continues past this transcript**
(paraphrase, not verbatim — [MY INTERPRETATION]): Abueloier doesn't
actually end up needing the heart. Juan puts Foolish's donated half-heart
into a salad instead of using it medically. As revenge, Foolish knocks
Juan down and, in exchange for helping him up, gets Juan to agree to give
up half of *his* heart in turn — then helps regrow it back at his kelp
lab. Foolish ends up carrying the other half of Juan's heart around in
his inventory afterward. In a later, not-yet-transcribed scene, Juan
tries to get it back by dressing up as a "personal robot assistant";
Foolish demands emeralds for it, so Juan eats the salad in front of him
instead. Around this same period, Juan and Foolish start a joint lab
venture to find a cure for radiation poisoning affecting the server.

## Transcript

Juan ("Doctor Atomico"), Abueloier, Nexe, and Foolish are in Foolish's
room, at the mini-lab Juan is treating as his own.

> **Juan:** "Well.. And, thats it. I'm working in science stuff with my.. Science mind."
> **Nexe:** "In Foolish room?"
> **Juan:** "Yeah, in my room."
> **Nexe:** "Yes."
> **Nexe:** "So Foolish this is also your laboratory?"
> **Juan:** "Yeah. He lives in my laboratory."
> **Foolish:** "No i do not."
> **Juan:** "Yeah. You need to pay the rent, bitch."
> **Foolish:** "Oh, here have a slice of chicken."
> **Juan:** "Oh.. Great, i love chicken… And science, because i'm a science guy."
> **Abueloier:** "Perfect science guy! help my grandpa!"
> **Juan:** "Oh yeah.. I need to cure grandpa."
> **Abueloier:** "yes, I'm gonna die. Rápido, güey"

They wait for the "doctor" to return with golden watermelon slices —
deemed unworkable and inedible — then potatoes, which also don't work.

> **Foolish:** "What did you do, doc?! He's dead!!"
> **Juan:** "Oh my God.. Oh my God."
> **Foolish:** "Fix him!"
> **Juan:** "Guys.. I have a bad news.. Maybe I gonna kill myself."
> **Foolish:** "Do it."
> **Nexe:** "You said that you have a cure!"
> **Foolish:** "All you did was feed him a potato!… Okay.. We might need to do a heart transplant, Nexe, how badly do you need your heart?"
> **Nexe:** "Eh.. I… Need? Period.."
> **Juan (Doctor Atomico):** "I know how to do it!" *(more confident than seconds earlier)*
> **Foolish:** "You know how to do a heart transplant?"
> **Juan:** "Yes.."
> **Foolish:** "Okay, here— Just take a part of mine. Okay, only half of it. I need at least half of my heart." *(lies down on the wooden floor)*
> **Juan:** "Okay."

*(The doctor starts to look hesitant.)*

> **Foolish:** "And then i'm sure, with my totem powers it'll just grow back.. So."
> **Juan:** "Okay.."
> **Foolish:** "Do you have like some numbing cream? heh.. For the pain?"
> **Juan:** "No, no. uh- I think that you are strong, you know?"
> **Foolish:** "Uh.. Okay. Just tell me when you're gonna do it—" *(squeezes eyes shut)* "AHHHHHHHHHHHH OHH IT HURTS, DOC— JUST GET IT OUT, JUST AHHHHHHH"
> **Juan:** "Ahh.. Corte del lado equivocado.."
> **Foolish:** "—THE WRONG SIDE??"
> **Juan:** "Yeah.. Fuck- Don't worry i can.." *(as Foolish wheezes)* "Just do the— Just do the other side-"

More back and forth — Foolish screaming, coughing, complaining; Juan
shouting at him to stay still while stitching. Finally, it's done.

> **Nexe:** "Are you okay?" *(helps him up)*
> **Foolish:** "huff.." *(breathing heavily, mostly intact)*
> **Juan:** "And I have one part of his heart."
> **Foolish:** "..Wait what?"
> **Nexe:** "Do you like to be a guinea pig or what is it??"
> **Foolish:** "Uh..huff, i dont know— I-i'm breathing? That's.. That's good."
> **Juan:** "Oh my God, I'm a scientist"
> **Foolish:** "Okay.. Uh, just make sure to put half my heart in grandpa."
> **Juan:** "Heh.. Que asco." *(holding it — strangely colored, inhuman)* "Uh.. Where is grandpa?"

---

Nexe eventually leaves; the two are alone. A couple hours later, Juan
calls Foolish to his room, holding a salad with a shit-eating grin.
Foolish knows before Juan can even say it.

> **Foolish:** "You didn't. you didn't, you did not… You. Did not just put my heart— into a salad. That's.. Correct."

*(A beat of silence.)*

> **Foolish:** "Doctor Atomico?"
> **Juan:** *(clears throat)* "Maybe.. I take a decision, science and food. My.. Two passions. Um.. I create, a heart salad."
> **Foolish:** "The heart salad.."
> **Juan:** "Yup."
> **Foolish:** "Man— You. Come over here" *(walks to the balcony)* "Do you ever just— take time and appreciate the view?"
> **Juan:** "Yeah. Is beautiful"
> **Foolish:** "You should um.. You should jump."

*(Juan stares.)*

> **Foolish:** "Just take it all in."
> **Juan:** "Why?" *(lightly laughing)*
> **Foolish:** "Why? Why? I just went through.. Excruciatingly painful heart surgery! JUST FOR YOU TO MAKE IT INTO A SALAD!!"
> **Juan:** "..Yeah. That's so value—"
> **Foolish:** "THE WHOLE POINT WAS TO CURE GRANDPA."
> **Juan:** "Thank you! You have science!"
> **Foolish:** "What is that even gonna do!??"
> **Juan:** "This salad is for grandpa!"

*(Foolish looks around, turns back.)*

> **Foolish:** "You know what? You know what?"
> **Juan:** "What?"
> **Foolish:** "I'll calm down.. Maybe, there's a world.. It works. I've seen more ridiculous things."
> **Juan:** "Yeah! A very good idea. El doctor atomico lo hizo de nuevo!"
> **Foolish:** "Hm.. Okay. Just a- Just a heart salad.. yeah.. No, that should do great. You just um- let me know how that goes.. And if it works, you will officially be a great scientist in my eyes."

*(Juan turns to face the balcony view, knife behind his back.)*

> **Foolish:** "What are you- You tryna like aura farm right now-? What are you even looking at?"
> **Juan:** "I'm thinking because I am smart."
> **Foolish:** "There's not a damn thought going on in there."
> **Juan:** "Uh.. I have a bad news for you."
> **Foolish:** "What do you mean? What now?"
> **Juan:** "I need that you leave my laboratory."
> **Foolish:** "What?"
> **Juan:** "Yeah."
> **Foolish:** "leave your laboratory? It's-"
> **Juan:** "Yup."
> **Foolish:** "Okay, one, it's my laboratory."
> **Juan:** "Umm.. No."
> **Foolish:** "Hm. Okay, well I think you should leave my barbecue." *(looks at the balcony grill)*
> **Juan:** "It's not your barbecue, it's my house."
> **Foolish:** "I think you should leave m-my barbecue." *(takes a knife from the cutting board)*
> **Juan:** "That not have sense—"
> **Foolish:** "Really? Cause this is my knife."
> **Juan:** "Oh, What the fuc-" *(coughing fit)* "I'm dying—"
> **Foolish:** "Oh, good. Good, I'm not gonna do anything about it."

*(Juan holds his chest, coughing — a morbid, unfamiliar thought crossing his mind, something he's been having whenever around the totem lately.)*

> **Juan:** "Put the knife— put the knife!"
> **Foolish:** "Okay. Yeah, i'll just-"

*(Juan steps back.)*

> **Juan:** "OW, What the fuck!??"
> **Foolish:** "What-? you said!"
> **Juan:** "What are you doing!??"
> **Foolish:** "I dont know! I was gonna-"

Juan swings at him with his own knife. A knife fight begins.

> **Juan:** "I'm doing surgery! I'm trying to help you! S-Sit still!! Sit still or its gonna be tougher to stitch you up! I'm just gonna take your heart.. And then your kidneys, and then your eyeballs. Yes, I'm gonna take your eyes. And then- and then I'm gonna make you into a lamp!" *(through breaths, groans, swings)*

Juan swings again anyway.

> **Foolish:** "Oh, Okay! I'm bringing out the big boy knife!" *(pulls out his sword)*
> **Juan:** "AHHHH AHHH"

> **Juan:** "ESTA LOCO!! AHHH OW-"

*(Juan lands on a patch of grass, having fallen off the stairs.)*

> **Foolish:** "Oh, you survived! How many levels you got?" *(kneels beside him)*

*(Foolish laughs, debating whether to help him.)*

> **Foolish:** "Uh- Give me your heart."
> **Juan:** "Agh… What??"

> **Juan:** "No! Fuck-? No!"
> **Foolish:** "Yeah- And then I wanna make a heart salad."
> **Juan:** "No- Uh, ten seconds, please FOOLISH, FIVE SECONDS"
> **Foolish:** "Just agree. Just agree that I get to have half your heart."
> **Juan:** "OKAY OKAY OKAYY"
> **Foolish:** "Okay! Perfect! Um.. Wait, I know- through my kelping scientific experiments, I know something to get you half of your heart back!"
> **Juan:** "What?"

> **Juan:** "How?"
> **Foolish:** "Come to MY lab."

He leads Juan to the kelp dragon.
