## Transcript

A few North members — Graf, Aldo, Juan, Foolish, Molly — have been
sitting in a circle around a beach campfire for about half an hour.
Waves, fire, crickets. The night breeze makes the fire feel warmer, at
least to Juan.

Graf and Aldo have already shared nice sentiments about each member
present. Graf is finishing up; Juan's turn is next. He's only a little
nervous — unusual, since he's never had trouble showing affection or
sharing his thoughts.

> **Graf:** "pero no tengo mucho de hablar, mucho de decir."
> **Juan:** "Qué bueno."
> **Molly:** "Gracias!"

Juan starts by reminding everyone of the amenities he'd prepared.

> **Juan:** "Por cierto, chicos, atrás hay pañuelos y sushi para el que necesite.—"
> **Graf:** "Perdóname por hablar tan mucho."
> **Juan:** "No, no, no, no. Vale,—"
> **Aldo:** "no, tu expláate."

Juan, thoughts already gathered:

> **Juan:** "Aldo. Admiro tu determinación, tu constancia, tu.. ¿cómo decirlo? Enganche a las cosas que te apasionan. Y sé que en algún momento vas a dirigir todos esos sentimientos… que a veces pueden parecer impulsivos a algo positivo.— Y eres muy importante para esta casa."

He continues, on a roll:

> **Juan:** "Molly, eres muy importante para la casa, trabajas demasiado, te estás equipando muy bien, aparte estás investigando por tu parte, tu aporte es valorado y tomado muy muy en cuenta."
> **Molly:** "¡Gracias!" *(applauds, drawing out the letters in a sing-song way)*
> **Aldo:** "¿Estás leyendo?" *(suspicious of how fast the words are coming)*
> **Juan:** "No, no, no, no. En verdad lo estoy diciendo.—Y foolish.."

Foolish, mid-mob-kill nearby, excuses himself and sits back down a moment later.

> **Foolish:** "Un momento."
> **Juan:** "Sí, sí, sí. Adelante."

He sits back down.

> **Juan:** "Folish you. Uh, foolish.—" *(makes a "wooo" sound, claps nervously)* "Right.. Foolish. You are like an…"
> **Foolish:** "Yeah?"
> **Juan:** "orange. You grew up in a tree."
> **Foolish:** "Okay.."
> **Juan:** "And you are like.. orange."
> **Foolish:** "Gracias?"
> **Juan:** "And you have a lot of.. Vitamina C. That's it. Ah, Te toca."
> **Foolish:** "Okay, thank you! That- That really meant a lot, i never knew you felt that way."
> **Juan:** "Muy bien, te toca."

*(Juan, internally: comparing Foolish to a fruit — not just any, his favorite. Qué idiota. But he had too many words to say, most of them bad, obviously. That was it — he couldn't think of anything because there was nothing good to say, and that's what he kept telling himself.)*

> **Foolish:** "Right. Graf, I really appreciate your- your visits or your time in the north. I feel like you're like way smarter than me every time and I feel like I become smarter talking to you."

*(Juan, before he can stop himself:)*

> **Juan:** "No, don't think so…"

*(The others give him a look; Foolish grins for half a second.)*

> **Juan:** "Sorry, sorry, sorry, sorry,"
> **Foolish:** "..Okay.. Aldo, I think your passion is contagious. I think I kind of want more of it seeing you defend and fight for the people of the north.. It makes me like want to start just like no question grab my sword and just start swinging. I think you're excellent generalismo supremo de… guerra? gyera.. gara? guerra de"
> **Aldo:** "guerra"
> **Graf:** "guerra."
> **Foolish:** "Si, Si.. Gracias."

*(He makes eye contact with Juan.)*

> **Foolish:** "Uh- I'll get to you later. Molly! you are so helpful. Similar to Graf where when I talk to you, it's like, 'Wow, you are just so much smarter than me.' Um, you help me a lot—"
> **Juan:** "Absolutely." *(can't help himself)*

*(What was up with him tonight? Foolish was actually trying to be vulnerable. Not that Juan hated the snarky comments — they gave him breathing room.)*

> **Foolish:** "—Around.. Well, like it like I can say it, but—"
> **Juan:** "Yeah. Yeah. Go ahead, sorry." *(as though he hadn't just interrupted purely to needle him)*

*(Foolish rolls his eyes dramatically.)*

> **Foolish:** "Um, you're really kind and patient. Um, you've also helped me a lot with my Spanish as well and I really appreciate it. Um, and and we got we got to go on like a little adventure soon, just like a little dungeon cuz I feel like you explore a lot and I haven I haven't done enough exploring… Neither has Juan. Juan doesn't explore at all."

*(Juan snorts humorlessly.)*

> **Foolish:** "Um Oh, ok. Finalmente, Juan.. Uh, even though sometimes I don't think you're like a super great uh segando almando plus plus.."

*(Juan scoffs; Foolish continues — unusual for him.)*

> **Foolish:** "l appreciate all the passion and the energy you bring to the house. It's an honor to get to live right under you. Uh I think we get to have a lot of fun and beautiful conversations. Um.. And I guess the only other thing I'll say is- please take back your camel."

*(For context: Juan's pet camel Camilla, whom he always puts in Foolish's room — see [EV-0040](../../01-canon-timeline/ev-0040-camila-camello.md).)*

> **Juan:** "Yeah. Thank you Foolish, that's beautiful. Thank you."

*(Juan almost feels guilty for saying so little now. Almost.)*

---

Later, after everyone's exchanged words:

> **Juan:** "Oficialmente la primera terapia rehabilitación retiro ha terminado y gracias por venir."

Everyone cheers, more laughs. Foolish, getting ready to leave:

> **Foolish:** "Thank you for organizing this was very-"
> **Juan:** "—Foolish! Can you stay one moment?"
> **Foolish:** "Oh! Yeah,"
> **Juan:** "Yeah, um.."
> **Foolish:** "Yeah."

Juan looks at Graf, still standing nearby.

> **Juan:** "Um.."
> **Graf:** "Ej, to było najlepsze, co zrobiłem na tym serwerze. Szczerze, dziękuję wam bardzo. Dziękuję wam bardzo." *(in Polish; Juan takes it as a sign of true gratitude)*
> **Juan:** "Oh, oh!"
> **Graf:** "Naprawdę się rozulam."
> **Juan:** "You're welcome!"
> **Foolish:** "Adios!"

*(A short pause — Juan watches Graf get out of earshot.)*

> **Juan:** "You are a good friend."
> **Foolish:** "Oh.. Thanks-"
> **Juan:** "I- I.. like live with you,"

*(Foolish giggles nervously.)*

> **Juan:** "And work with you- and.. I enjoy the time with you, and you are a- i appreciate.. You."

*(Foolish looks stupidly pleased, responds in broken Spanish.)*

> **Foolish:** "Oh, Y yo tambien disfruta tu? disfruuta tu?" *(asking for clarification the sentence is correct — it's not)*
> **Juan:** "T- Eh.. Disfruto tu compañía, se podría decir." *(chuckling)*

*(They both laugh.)*

> **Foolish:** "Oh, disfruto tu.. comp- compen-"
> **Juan:** "Compañía"
> **Foolish:** "compañar compan-"

*(Juan gets slightly irritated — Foolish is good at that.)*

> **Juan:** "COMPAÑÍA!, COMP-AÑ-ÍA"
> **Foolish:** "COMPAÑÍA!"
> **Juan:** "yes, yes. Good work, Foolish"

*(Foolish giggles, having had an awesomely evil thought.)*

> **Foolish:** "I'm gonna tell them you said all those things—"
> **Juan:** "no, no, no, no—"
> **Foolish:** "Si, si"
> **Juan:** "No, you don't dont say anything about this—"
> **Foolish:** "Si, si, no mas palabras, no mas palabras—" *(teasing, dancing playfully)*
> **Juan:** "Go to work!"
> **Foolish:** "Si, si, estoy trabajo!"

He walks off; Juan starts packing up the day's setup.
