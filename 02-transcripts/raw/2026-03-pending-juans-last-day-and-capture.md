## Transcript

Juan was excited to meet his so-called secret admirer. He really was. He
put on his best suit, brought a gift. It was odd that Federation workers
were the ones delivering the message.

After the North's meeting, doubts started creeping in — worse after
seeing a private message from Quackity, sent right before he logged off:

> Quackity te susurró: AMIDO PUDEES VER MI MENSAJE?
> Quackity te susurró: AMIGO NO VAYAS A ESA REUNION
> susurraste a Quackity: si
> Quackity te susurró: NO SE QUE ME ESTA PASANDO

But more than anything, Juan wished to feel loved — not by that damned
Cucurucho, not anymore, but by his family, The North, the ones he'd be
loyal to forever. This appointment, whatever it turned out to be, would
be proof of that. He didn't tell anyone about it; it would only worry
them, and Foolish especially would want to tag along. They were always
together — even earlier that day Juan had agreed to Foolish's plan to
bait the entity, and Foolish had trusted him enough to bring him into
the secret Federation-infiltration plan. This felt different. It was a
date, after all — who brings friends to a date?

Who was he kidding — he was terrified. He didn't have Aldo's military
endurance or Foolish's immortality; most of his days were spent playing
"office simulator" in the castle. He couldn't back out now — the
consequences of not showing would likely be worse. He'd keep playing
dumb, feign ignorance. Bliss. Just preparing for a date, that's all.

He needed to talk to someone first. Foolish was the worst candidate — and
also the one he needed. As second in command plus plus, right below him,
Foolish would be left in charge. They belittled each other constantly,
and Juan would call him a lazy egocentric bastard with total conviction,
but he didn't actually believe it — the kelp dragon alone disproved it,
to say nothing of how Foolish had won over nearly everyone on the server.
Even him. Though he'd never admit it.

His talk with Aldo after the meeting was short:

> **Aldo:** "Bueno. Mi mayor arma era senpai pero no lo tengo ahorita. Cuando quieras, y ya hagas los tuyo.. prepara la gente para tener un PVP training de PVP pequeño, porque lo vamos a necesitar con todo que viene."
> **Juan:** "Esta bien."

Juan starts back to his office, calling out: "Foolish, come to my office please."

Foolish, mid-conversation elsewhere, doesn't rush — Juan calling him over
is usually annoying, occasionally mildly important, never urgent. He
finishes his conversation, laughs at something, lets the moment stretch.
Eventually he wanders over, doesn't bother knocking.

> **Foolish:** "Hello!"

Juan immediately stands, opens the door to his adjoined room instead —
personal, not work.

> **Juan:** "Come in! No wait, come here. I don't want to nobody listen."

Foolish follows, closes the distance without thinking about it — for a
split second wonders, *is he gonna push me off,* and almost says it out
loud, grinning.

> **Juan:** "Okay. The situation is so fucked up, you know it?"

Foolish deflects, playful: "You look like you have a great idea right now."

> **Juan:** "No, no. I just want to say something."

Foolish actually looks at him then — Juan seems nervous, the same
nervousness from right after the North's rehabilitation retreat, when
he'd asked Foolish to stay back and said all those things he couldn't
say in front of everyone. *Ah, so that's what this is about,* Foolish
thinks, something tightening in his chest.

> **Juan:** "If something happens in this situation with the regime, the federation, or whatever, remember that you're second in command plus plus, okay?— This house needs you like needs me, like needs vegetta, like needs molly like needs everybody, okay?"
> **Foolish:** "…Yeah"

Foolish knows that already — it's his role, being below Juan, less
responsibility, even if he talks about wanting a promotion. He likes it
this way.

> **Juan:** "I believe in you. A lot"

Not something Juan usually says outright. Foolish shifts, taps the
railing once. He could say something real back.

> **Foolish:** "I believe in you a little bit."

A coward's answer, but it lands exactly as intended — Juan chuckles. Back to normal.

> **Juan:** "It's okay for me.. Thats it, i just need to remind you."

That almost pulls Foolish back into it. He keeps it surface-level instead.

> **Foolish:** "Thank you."

A pause — he almost means to finish the thought.

> **Foolish:** "You know, i really appreciate that Juan—"

Juan cuts it off — he's running out of time.

> **Juan:** "Now go to work, motherfucker"

Relief hits Foolish faster than it should.

> **Foolish:** "—Segunda almando Deluxe!"
> **Juan:** "Thank you! Now, I have something to do."
> **Foolish:** "Okay!—"
> **Juan:** "Bye!"

Juan walks toward his chests, the earlier smile fading only slightly. He
talks to himself for comfort:

> **Juan:** ".. flor dime que tengo la flor.. ah, no tengo una flor pero tengo un regalo!"

He passes Tomate, a lower-tier Federation camera operator assigned to follow inhabitants around.

> **Juan:** "Hola tomate… no tengo tiempo para hablar ahora" ... "Tengo un admirador secreto..! sabías eso?—"

He sits on his balcony bench.

> **Juan:** "esta vez espero que no me dejen plantada. seria bastante triste seria basura, la verdad. Pero esta vez va a ser diferente." *(mostly for the camera)*

Another, higher-tier Federation worker drops him a book, which he reads aloud:

> **Book:** "vente conmigo No hagas preguntas, deja es a toda tus cosas.."

Senpai Spider walks in, looking dejected.

> **Senpai Spider:** "I need a hug. I need a hug"

Juan switches immediately into comforting mode — something he does often as second in command, mirroring Vegetta, especially with newer members.

> **Juan:** "sure, sure, sure man, Sure, the Little Prince! everybody gonna be okay!"

They hug briefly; Senpai backs out, nodding.

> **Senpai Spider:** "Alright, Alright. That was all, sorry."

---

Directly afterward, Juan puts away his things — just the gift and his teddy bear stay with him.

> **Juan (to the Federation worker):** "..y listo." ... "porque—"

He almost asks why, as the worker sets down a boat to teleport them. Only one thing that could mean — this wouldn't be close by. Not weird, he tells himself. The North's base is far from everyone else anyway.

> **Juan:** "..no hago preguntas.. Me dejo sorprender."

They arrive at a minimally decorated, round white waiting room.

> **Juan:** "y esto..? un restaurante elegante?"

He's hit once by the worker for lingering — takes it as a cue to keep moving. Turns to the camera operator following:

> **Juan:** "porque los mandaron a ustedes, estan en servicio de citas tambien?.. esa música.."

He reaches a door, walks in mid-sentence — a smaller, equally white copy of the room before.

> **Juan:** "un poco como de espa." *(looking around — plants, shelves, a locked door)* "tómate que está pasando aqui.. … tomate?"

Cucurucho appears from the door. Juan can't even back away.

> **Juan:** "…cucurucho"
> **Cucurucho:** "Hola Juan."

*(Cucurucho is creepier alone with him than Robleis ever was in disguise.)*

> **Juan:** "Hola."
> **Cucurucho:** "hasta que Aldo coopere o cumpla lo acordado, no serás liberado" ... "Espero que disfrutes de la isla"
> **Juan:** "me vas a dejar aquí hasta que Aldo-"

He doesn't finish. Wither skeletons wearing Cucurucho's face swarm the room; Juan runs, screaming, before blacking out:

> **Juan:** "qué verga te pasa idiota! No mames! No mames!" ... "Aldo lo va acer!"
