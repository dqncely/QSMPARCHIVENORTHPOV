# REV-0007 — Circumstances of Juan's disappearance

- **Revelation ID:** REV-0007
- **Origin event:** [EV-0033](../01-canon-timeline/ev-0033-juan-disappearance.md); developed through [EV-0049](../01-canon-timeline/ev-0049-juan-captivity-days-1-6.md) onward.
- **Certainty level (overall):** Confirmed as reported; several mechanics (why this specific method, what "cooperation" ultimately requires) remain open.

## The information

Juan was lured away by a fake "secret admirer" invitation, delivered by Federation workers, and taken to Cucurucho, who is holding him captive until Aldo "cooperates" — completing a Federation-assigned task (originally: kill every Regime member by Monday). Juan is held alone in a sparse white room, attacked by withers on at least two occasions, and has his teddy bear confiscated and later used as a psychological pressure tool against Aldo directly. Aldo ultimately turns himself in rather than complete the task, which the Federation rejects as insufficient (it specifically needed to be Foolish who did the killing, not Aldo) — meaning, as of the last tracked event, Juan's actual path back to the North remains unresolved.

## Who they've told

The North's core group (Foolish, Aldo, Roier, Alondra, later Tina) all know by [EV-0053](../01-canon-timeline/ev-0053-aldo-sacrifice-tina-foolish-mirror.md). Not shown as disclosed outside the North as of the last tracked event, beyond what's implied by island-wide "tragedy" framing per author.

## Who incorrectly believes something else

- The wider island initially receives only the staged/false reports of Fariis's and Foolish's "murders" as unrelated infiltrator incidents — not connected publicly to Juan's kidnapping. [CANON FACT, EV-0051]

## Who still doesn't know

- The exact terms of what would actually secure Juan's release are unclear even to Aldo and Foolish as of the last tracked event — the Federation rejected Aldo's self-sacrifice specifically because it had to be Foolish doing the killing, and that condition hasn't been met.
- What happened to Quackity and whether he ever received Juan's Day 5 message is not resolved.
