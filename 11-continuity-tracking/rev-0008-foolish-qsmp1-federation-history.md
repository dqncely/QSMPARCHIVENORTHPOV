# REV-0008 — Foolish's QSMP1 Federation history ("employee of the month")

- **Revelation ID:** REV-0008
- **Origin event:** Referenced at [EV-0045](../01-canon-timeline/ev-0045-maximus-oh-moment.md); the history itself predates the tracked QSMP2 timeline entirely.
- **Certainty level (overall):** Mixed — the fact that this history exists and is why Maximus/other QSMP1 veterans distrust Foolish is [CANON FACT, per author]; specifics of what actually happened are not detailed.

## The information

On QSMP1, Foolish did something comparable to his current Federation double-agent plan with Juan ([EV-0030](../01-canon-timeline/ev-0030-federation-recruitment-arc.md)): he went undercover for the Federation, ended up with "blood on his hands," and was even named the Federation's "employee of the month." Foolish himself draws the direct comparison to Juan's "office simulator" habit — implying he sees the parallel and isn't hiding from it, even if the details aren't spelled out to Juan (or to us) yet.
