## Continuity cautions

- QNN posting time establishes report order, not necessarily the exact minute an event occurred.

- Dark Cucurucho's statement that Cucurucho is his brother remains an attributed claim.
- Juan's June 9 status remains “reported dead” until a later direct source explains the outcome.
- Aldo's later agency remains unresolved because the archive already establishes captivity and brainwashing. His actions should not automatically be written as wholly voluntary.
- The complete export contains many additional side-character and faction events. They remain available for later focused passes rather than being collapsed into the Juan/Foolish-centered timeline without narrative context.
