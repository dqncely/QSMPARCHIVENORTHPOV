# REV-0006 — Who's actually responsible for Estupidog's death

- **Revelation ID:** REV-0006
- **Origin event:** [EV-0009](../01-canon-timeline/ev-0009-foolish-estupidog-confession.md) (Foolish's confession), contradicted by [EV-0010](../01-canon-timeline/ev-0010-juan-tubbo-office-meeting.md) vs. [EV-0015](../01-canon-timeline/ev-0015-ash-battle-estupidog-confrontation.md)
- **Certainty level (overall):** Deliberately unresolved — this entry exists to track a genuine, unreconciled contradiction rather than to state a fact.

## The information

Three different, mutually inconsistent accounts exist:
1. Foolish confesses (under psychological pressure from Juan) to having collaborated with the Regime to kill Estupidog for a promotion. [CANON FACT — EV-0009]
2. Tubbo tells Juan he only observed and didn't participate, and that he loves dogs/felt bad. [CANON FACT — EV-0010]
3. Ash tells The North that *he* killed Estupidog because Tubbo ordered him to. [CANON FACT — EV-0015]

These cannot all be fully true as stated. Not resolved by the source; preserved as unresolved.

## Who they've told

- Foolish told Juan (EV-0009). Ash told The North broadly (EV-0015). Tubbo told Juan his version (EV-0010).

## Who incorrectly believes something else

Genuinely unknown which party (if any) is lying — flagged rather than assumed. Juan, having heard both Tubbo's and (later) Ash's accounts, is in the best position in-universe to notice the contradiction; whether he does is not shown.
