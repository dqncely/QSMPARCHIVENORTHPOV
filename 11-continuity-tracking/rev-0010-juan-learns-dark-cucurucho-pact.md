# REV-0010 — Juan learns about the Dark Cucurucho soul pact

- **Revelation ID:** REV-0010
- **Origin event:** [EV-0064](../01-canon-timeline/ev-0064-foolish-shows-juan-temple.md)
- **Certainty level (overall):** Confirmed.

## The information

Foolish tells Juan the full story of the temple, Dark Cucurucho, and the
risk that completing it will cost him his soul. This **updates and
partially reverses** the earlier assumption (recorded in
[REV-0007](rev-0007-juan-disappearance-circumstances.md) and the
[Q!Foolish & Q!Juan](../04-relationship-sheets/q-foolish-and-q-juan.md)
relationship sheet) that Juan had no knowledge of the pact. As of
[EV-0064](../01-canon-timeline/ev-0064-foolish-shows-juan-temple.md), he
does — and rejects the soul-cost outright, wanting an alternative plan.
