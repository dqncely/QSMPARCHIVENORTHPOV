## The information

[MY INTERPRETATION — narration, not shown on-screen] Foolish was on the
QSMP1 island and survived "purgatory." He has direct history with that
island and is one of the only people who actually remembers what
happened there. This cruise/island situation is described as "eerily
similar" to his prior QSMP1 experience.
