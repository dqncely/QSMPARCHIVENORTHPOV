# REV-0005 — "Cucurucho" was Robleis in disguise

- **Revelation ID:** REV-0005
- **Origin event:** [EV-0031](../01-canon-timeline/ev-0031-cucurucho-robleis-reveal.md) (reveal); roots in [EV-0007](../01-canon-timeline/ev-0007-juan-awaits-cucurucho.md)/[EV-0008](../01-canon-timeline/ev-0008-cucurucho-appears-off-robleis-vanishes.md)
- **Certainty level (overall):** Mixed — the disappearance/appearance coincidence (EV-0008) is [CANON FACT]; the TCG-card reveal itself (EV-0031) is [MY INTERPRETATION], pending QNN date confirmation.

## The information

Juan's romantic/social encounters with "Cucurucho" were, at least at some point, actually Q!Robleis in disguise — done as an attempt to comfort Juan after the real Cucurucho stood him up, not as a cruel prank. Juan believed there was mutual interest between himself and Cucurucho, and even believed he'd ended things himself after Cucurucho's attacks on The North — none of which involved the real Cucurucho.
