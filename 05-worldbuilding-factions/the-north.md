# The North

- **Leader:** Q!Vegetta777 (CH-005)
- **Territory:** North castle/mansion, adjacent to Q!Foolish's purple dragon build and his kelpamine lab
- **Status:** Active
- **Canon status:** Mixed — structural facts (roles, membership) are [CANON FACT] per the QNN recap source and author-provided context; some role details are the author's own understanding rather than a direct quote.

## Canon facts

### Hierarchy

- Q!Vegetta777 — leader/patriarch of The North. [CANON FACT]
- Q!Aldo (CH-006) — "Generalísimo," responsible for war/conflict decisions. [CANON FACT]
- Q!Tina (CH-004) — below Aldo in a similar war-response role. [MY INTERPRETATION — author's understanding, not a direct quote]
- Q!Juan (CH-003) — "Segundo al mando deluxe" — runs the North's inner workings: meeting planning, day-to-day logistics, diplomacy with other factions when Vegetta is absent. [CANON FACT]
- Q!Foolish (CH-001) — "Segundo al mando plus plus" — directly under Juan in the hierarchy. [CANON FACT]
- Q!Molly (CH-007) — considered the princess of The North. [MY INTERPRETATION — author's understanding]
- Q!Roier (CH-002) — possibly the prince, unconfirmed by the author themselves. [MY INTERPRETATION — explicitly uncertain per author]

### Membership

- Vegetta's biological/adopted children (from before QSMP2): Q!Roier, Q!Juan, Q!Aldo, Q!Molly. [CANON FACT / author-stated]
- Other main household inhabitants: Q!Tina, Q!Foolish. [CANON FACT]
- Q!Foolish was invited into the household specifically due to history with Vegetta from QSMP1 (see [Q!Foolish & Q!Vegetta777](../04-relationship-sheets/q-foolish-and-q-vegetta777.md)). [CANON FACT — invitation itself; MY INTERPRETATION for the "why" beyond what's stated]
- Newer/younger-coded additions: Pac (recently adopted), Senpai Spider. [MY INTERPRETATION — author's read that these two are treated as more "childlike" than the founding four]
- Also affiliated, without the same "youthful" framing: Alondrissa, Maximus. [MY INTERPRETATION]
- Q!Quackity has been loosely affiliated with The North but doesn't live there like the others, and isn't fully considered "part of" the group by some members — a source of insecurity for him. [MY INTERPRETATION — author's read, tied to [REV pending] his conversation with Juan about it]

### Culture / recurring traditions

- Weekly events organized primarily by Juan: "Barbecue Mondays" and "Purple Wednesday" (the latter a homage to Vegetta). [CANON FACT]
- Karaoke nights, North conferences (formal meetings, usually run for/by Aldo). [CANON FACT]
- Juan sings around the house while working, is giggly/smiley by default. [CANON FACT — author-observed pattern]
- Foolish nicknames Juan's office-management habit "office simulator." [CANON FACT]
