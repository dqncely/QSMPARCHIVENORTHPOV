# The Regime (El Regimen)

- **Notable members:** Q!Ashswag (CH-008), Q!Tubbo (CH-009), Pac ("Fofoquero" — actually The North's, see note)
- **Territory:** Regime zone (separate from The North's territory; has a train connecting to/through it)
- **Status:** Active; in an escalating, on-again-off-again conflict with [The North](the-north.md)
- **Canon status:** Mixed — membership/actions are [CANON FACT] per the QNN recap source; internal structure is thin so far.

## Canon facts

- Attempted to recruit Q!Foolish early on; he declined, citing his existing team within The North. [CANON FACT]
- Q!Ash later admitted to killing Estupidog because Q!Tubbo asked him to, framing it as following orders he's obligated to carry out for the Regime. [CANON FACT]
- Conflict with The North has included: a mine detonation, a direct physical battle (Ash vs. Aldo and Juan), Ash stealing items from Aldo afterward, and a train-theft incident blamed on the Regime being a distraction during peace talks. [CANON FACT]
- Also attempted at least one real peace overture: Ash met with Juan, expressed wanting peace, and the meeting ended with Juan giving him a "flower of peace." Note: per the QNN dated source, this peace/flower moment is timestamped shortly *after* the battle described above, on the same day — inconsistent with the order these were originally described to me in. **Flagged, not resolved** — see [EV-00xx] for both events once filed.
