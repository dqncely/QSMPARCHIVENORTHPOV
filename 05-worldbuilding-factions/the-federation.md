# The Federation

- **Notable figures:** [Cucurucho](../03-character-sheets/cucurucho.md), [Dark Cucurucho](../03-character-sheets/dark-cucurucho.md) (relationship between the two unresolved), [Multi](../03-character-sheets/multi.md) (runs a lab), Tomate and other camera/worker roles ([Tomate](../03-character-sheets/tomate.md), Pepino, Coco, Funny Dance Guy, Funny News Guy — the latter four not yet individually detailed)
- **Status:** Active, and — per confirmed events — deliberately manipulating both [The North](the-north.md) and [The Regime](the-regime.md) into conflict with each other rather than favoring either side.
- **Canon status:** Mixed.

## Canon facts

- Predates QSMP2 — Foolish was Federation-affiliated on QSMP1, with a history involving "blood on his hands" and being named "employee of the month." See [REV-0008](../11-continuity-tracking/rev-0008-foolish-qsmp1-federation-history.md).
- Runs the "E.G.G.S." program referenced in QSMP1-era lore (the eggs, including Foolish and Vegetta's Leonarda, were put into cryogenic sleep by the Federation in 2024).
- Offered Foolish a position in QSMP2 ([EV-0030](../01-canon-timeline/ev-0030-federation-recruitment-arc.md)); Foolish brought Juan into the plan shortly after ([EV-0047](../01-canon-timeline/ev-0047-rooftop-cacahuatazo.md)).
- Simultaneously offered Q!Fariis (a Regime member) a reward for killing Foolish — confirming the Federation arms/uses both North and Regime against each other rather than backing either. [CANON FACT, EV-0051]
- Used a fake "secret admirer" invitation to lure Juan into captivity, holding him hostage to pressure Aldo into attacking the Regime. [CANON FACT, EV-0033 onward]
- Multi's lab, affiliated with [Polish Cave](polish-cave.md) rather than the Federation directly, was responsible for extracting Foolish's DNA under still-unexplained circumstances. [CANON FACT — see [EV-0025](../01-canon-timeline/ev-0025-foolish-dna-extraction-confession.md)]
- Runs a temple-building/soul-binding ritual through Dark Cucurucho, which Foolish accepts in [EV-0055](../01-canon-timeline/ev-0055-dark-cucurucho-ritual.md) — unclear whether this is an official Federation operation or something adjacent/separate.
