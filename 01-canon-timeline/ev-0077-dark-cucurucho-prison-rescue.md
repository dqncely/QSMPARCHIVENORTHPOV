# EV-0077 — Dark Cucurucho's prisoners and the failed rescue

## Identity

- **Event ID:** EV-0077
- **Date:** 2026-06-12 through 2026-06-16 [CANON FACT, QNN recap sequence]
- **Certainty level:** Confirmed at recap level; this entry groups one continuous captivity/rescue arc.

## Event

Dark Cucurucho interrogates Pepino and threatens Tomate. Pepino reveals that the Federation produced his voice experimentally and creates clones through DNA extraction. Dark Cucurucho claims to be developing increasingly aggressive clones and an army of copies of himself. Pepino and Tomate are tortured, briefly escape across dimensions, and are recaptured. [CANON FACT]

Foolish later shows Tubbo a prison he built for Dark Cucurucho and discovers Pepino and Tomate already confined there. Foolish says he only constructed the prison and did not know who its prisoners would be, while also expressing little immediate objection. [CANON FACT]

Nacho is subsequently taken into the same conflict. Multi and Quackity mount a rescue with Katie distracting Dark Cucurucho. They free Nacho, Tomate, and Pepino, but Fatal Error and Dark workers recapture the group before Federation forces intervene. Cucurucho allows most captives to leave but retains Quackity and Pepino. [CANON FACT]

In Federation custody, Quackity rejects freedom conditioned on working for the Federation and risking Pepino. Multi's arrival creates an opening for Pepino to escape and exposes to Quackity that Multi has a collaborative relationship with the Federation. [CANON FACT]
