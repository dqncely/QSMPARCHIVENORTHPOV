# EV-0069 — Juan stalls the second date and recruits Abueloier

## Identity

- **Event ID:** EV-0069
- **Date:** Unknown — the day after EV-0067/EV-0068
- **Certainty:** Dialogue/actions are [CANON FACT]; explanations for Foolish’s absence are unresolved

## Event

Juan paints Roier at Mi Arte while repeatedly delaying his promised second date with Graf. He claims Foolish has his lucky date shirt and is needed as his fashion police, date planner, and wingman. When Graf secures an outfit from Katie, Juan invents a phone call from Foolish and continues waiting. Foolish never arrives; the source does not establish why.

Graf gives Juan a large white bow supplied by Katie, who believes the date is with a girl called Juanita. Juan then finds Abueloier, delivers Roier’s painting, and confesses the situation. He states plainly that Graf is a dear friend but he is not romantically interested and is afraid to love again after his prior heartbreak. Abueloier advises honesty. Juan instead recruits him to attend and sabotage the date by behaving senile.

## Consequences

- Abueloier becomes Juan’s substitute wingman and chaperone.
- Katie remains unaware that “Juanita” is Juan.
- Foolish’s absence becomes a genuine continuity gap; do not fill it as canon without evidence.
