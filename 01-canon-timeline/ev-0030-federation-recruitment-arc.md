## Summary

The Federation approaches Foolish to become a member. He tells Juan first — the first person he tells — framing it as potentially beneficial, and mentioning he was a Federation member on the first island (QSMP1). Juan initially reacts badly, calling him a traitor. They eventually bring it to Aldo; in that conversation, Juan reverses course and defends Foolish, even when Aldo isn't fully on board, as do other North members. Juan later tells Maximus (also a QSMP1 veteran) because he thought he could trust him — but Maximus tells others. Foolish reacts badly to the leak; Juan sincerely apologizes, which the author notes is rare for him. Afterward, alone, Juan tells Foolish he trusts him fully. Foolish invites Juan to join him in the Federation dealings so they can gather information together; Juan is reluctant, given the greater personal risk, but agrees. ## Detailed sequence of events

1. Federation approaches Foolish about membership.
2. Foolish tells Juan first, framing it as potentially useful; mentions his QSMP1 Federation history.
3. Juan initially reacts with anger, calls him a traitor.
4. They bring it to Aldo; Juan reverses and defends Foolish even against Aldo's hesitation, as do other North members.
5. Juan tells Maximus in confidence; Maximus leaks it to others.
6. Foolish is angry about the leak; Juan sincerely apologizes — notably rare for him.
7. Alone together, Juan tells Foolish he trusts him fully.
8. Foolish invites Juan to join him in gathering Federation-related information; Juan, reluctant given the added risk to himself, agrees.

## Consequences

- Major trust-building arc directly relevant to the "how their trust was built over time" question this whole timeline exists to answer — arguably the single densest trust-development event on record so far.
- Establishes Juan's capacity for sincere apology as genuinely rare, making this instance notable.
- Sets up ongoing joint Federation-information-gathering as an activity they now do together, deepening their working relationship beyond North duties.
