## Summary

Foolish gives Juan a free sample of kelpamine — his first-ever "sale," though it cost nothing; he was just testing the waters, and Juan happened to be the first person he ran into after finishing his first batch. Juan drinks it immediately (surprising Foolish with how quickly he agrees), goes invisible, and reports feeling dizzy and "flying." He doesn't end up enjoying the experience — unlike Aldo, who becomes a consistent customer — but finds it interesting nonetheless. [CANON FACT]
