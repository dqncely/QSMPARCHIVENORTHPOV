## Summary

Both leave the island for roughly a week under the island's standing leave policy (see Consequences) — Foolish to a wedding, Juan to a championship where he served as an advisor; his team wins. He's returned extremely drunk, in a random location rather than home, and summons Foolish (back a day earlier) to meet him. What follows is a loose, affectionate, very drunk exchange: Foolish deflecting a mine-related mishap onto an absurd "ant invasion" story that Juan finds delighted rather than convinced by, a lapse into their Star Wars/temple codeword, and Foolish sincerely congratulating Juan's team win. The two then discover Cucurucho Land, a new amusement park near spawn, and spend a long stretch performing mutual disinterest and disgust at Cucurucho branding while circling back toward it out of curiosity anyway — including Foolish's poorly-defended claim that his one visit was purely "enemy surveillance," which Juan doesn't buy for a second. [CANON FACT]

## Consequences

- Establishes a general island mechanic, per author: members get limited leave for events (weddings, vacations for popular members like Katie B.), but are required back — sometimes without memory of the return — specifically to avoid suspicion from any real-world connections members might have outside the island. Worth using as a standing explanation for future unexplained absences, rather than re-explaining each time.
- Cucurucho Land introduces a new, recurring in-universe location, and confirms Foolish has already visited once without Juan.
