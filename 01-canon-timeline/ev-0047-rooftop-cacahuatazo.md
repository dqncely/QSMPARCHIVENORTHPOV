## Summary

Juan seeks Foolish out at his dragon build; the two use their private codeword ("cacahuatazo") to discuss the Federation plan properly. Foolish proposes a fix — claim his "cover was blown" and quietly return to only the core group knowing. He then asks Juan to move somewhere more discreet; they teleport somewhere high up, overlooking the island. There, Foolish reveals he gave Juan's name to a Federation worker as another potential recruit. Juan, uncharacteristically, admits he's scared — citing Maximus's anger and Aldo's confusion. Foolish tries to reassure him with an idiom Juan doesn't parse ("pantalones grandes"), which turns into an unintentionally charged bit about Juan's pants/fitness before Foolish clarifies. They settle on including only Aldo and Tina, at most, in the secret — Foolish notes Juan's involvement adds a useful "backup layer" since no one suspects him the way they might suspect Foolish.
