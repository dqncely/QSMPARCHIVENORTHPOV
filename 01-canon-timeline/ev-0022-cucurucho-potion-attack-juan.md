## Summary

While working at the mansion on Vegetta's orders, Juan is suddenly confronted by Cucurucho, who throws a potion that clouds his vision; two black entities then attack him in an apparent attempt to kill him. Frightened and confused, Juan seeks out Aldo, who shows him videos/photos of his own recent experiences with Cucurucho — leaving Juan even more disturbed. [CANON FACT]

## Consequences

- Escalates Cucurucho from "unreliable romantic interest" to an active physical threat — worth holding both readings simultaneously given the still-unresolved Cucurucho/Robleis identity question (see [Cucurucho](../03-character-sheets/cucurucho.md), [REV-0005](../11-continuity-tracking/rev-0005-cucurucho-is-robleis.md)).
