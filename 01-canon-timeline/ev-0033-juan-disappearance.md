## Summary

Juan believes he's going on a date with a secret admirer — a Federation-delivered invitation he's genuinely excited about, suit and gift prepared, despite a warning from Quackity right before Quackity logs off ("AMIGO NO VAYAS A ESA REUNION"). Underneath the excitement is real anxiety: Juan doesn't have Aldo's combat ability or Foolish's immortality, and reasons his way into going anyway — backing out now seems more dangerous than showing up, and he'd rather not worry anyone, especially Foolish, who'd insist on tagging along. He tells no one where he's actually going.

Before he leaves, he calls Foolish into his office for what becomes their real goodbye, though neither names it that way. Juan tells him plainly that the house needs him, that he believes in him — Foolish, uncharacteristically, almost lets himself say something equally sincere back before both retreat into banter and a "go to work, motherfucker" send-off. Juan then spends his last free minutes talking to himself, getting a hug from Senpai Spider ("the Little Prince," who needed comfort himself), and packing only a gift and his teddy bear. A Federation worker escorts him by boat to a distant, sterile white waiting room, then a smaller room where Cucurucho appears in person — colder and more unsettling than even Robleis's impersonation had been. Cucurucho tells him he won't be freed until Aldo "cooperates" with an unspecified arrangement. Before Juan can finish asking what that means, wither skeletons wearing Cucurucho's face attack him; he blacks out shouting that Aldo will do it.

## Detailed account

The chest-room meeting with Foolish is the emotional center of this event: Juan frames it as reassurance about the North's stability, Foolish reads it (correctly, per the author) as an echo of Juan's earlier private confession after the rehabilitation retreat ([EV-0028](ev-0028-juan-foolish-private-confession.md)). Both come close to real vulnerability — Foolish starts to say "you know, i really appreciate that Juan—" — and both pull back before finishing, Juan cutting the moment off first by sending him to work. Neither of them treats this as a goodbye at the time. [CANON FACT for the dialogue; MY INTERPRETATION for reading it as an unrecognized goodbye]

Juan's solo scenes afterward (talking to himself about the flower he doesn't have but the gift he does, reading a note delivered by a Federation worker, hugging Senpai Spider) establish his emotional state clearly: hopeful, performing confidence for an audience of exactly no one who knows what's really happening, genuinely trying to project calm. [MY INTERPRETATION for the emotional read; CANON FACT for the recorded actions/dialogue]

The capture itself is abrupt after a long, quiet buildup — Cucurucho's brief, cold dialogue, then an immediate switch to violence with no further explanation. The wither attack is the same method used against Juan earlier in the tracked timeline (see [EV-0022](ev-0022-cucurucho-potion-attack-juan.md)), now escalated to a captivity scenario rather than a standalone scare.
