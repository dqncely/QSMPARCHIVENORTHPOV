## Summary

Juan receives a note from a Federation worker informing him of an important 7:00 PM appointment. The note asks him to be on his room's balcony at that time, alone. Juan follows the instructions, preparing with enthusiasm for the appointment. At the indicated time, a Federation worker arrives to escort him, asking him to leave all his belongings and follow without asking questions. Juan complies. Since then, no one on the island has heard from him, and there has been no further contact. [CANON FACT, per report style]

Separately: right before leaving for the "date," Juan called Foolish to come talk — the last person he spoke with, apart from Ishan, who came in shortly after for a hug. [MY INTERPRETATION — author-added context, pending confirmation]
