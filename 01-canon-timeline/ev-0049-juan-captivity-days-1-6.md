## Summary

Juan is held in a featureless white room, with almost no contact — a guard on Day 3 to confiscate his teddy bear, another on Day 6 to cut off an attempted message. Left otherwise alone, he talks to himself and to the teddy for company, cycling through denial, boredom, dread, and flashes of anger. He worries specifically about whether Quackity was also taken, guilty that he'd ignored Quackity's warning. Withers attack him at least twice during this stretch — after the teddy is confiscated, and again after he's caught trying to message someone. By Day 4 he starts covering the doors with picture frames so he can't be watched; by Day 6 he's reduced to a single desperate attempt to reach Foolish through a chat box before a guard interrupts him. [CANON FACT]

Read together, the six days trace a fairly specific emotional arc: Day 1's shock and disbelief, Day 2's flat waiting, Day 3's confiscation and the first real note of desperation, Day 4's defiant anger (refusing to be anyone's "payaso"), Day 5's guilt and fear specifically for Quackity, Day 6's collapse into simply wanting contact with anyone, landing on Foolish's name specifically.
