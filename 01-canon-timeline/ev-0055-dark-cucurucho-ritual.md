## Summary

Foolish receives coordinates far off the map — later revealed as a prank from Aldo, meant to lure him back to the castle, but by the time that's known it's already pulled him deeper into the search rather than back home. His first attempt to reach the coordinates ends in death from a rare kelpamine side effect, forcing him to restock and start over. What he eventually finds is an unfinished, deliberately unsettling temple, with build instructions posted nearby: complete the structure spanning bedrock to sky, build a waystone, summon whoever posted the instructions, and surrender his soul. By this point — Aldo gone, Juan still missing, weeks of forest isolation and mounting kelpamine use behind him — Foolish decides there's nothing left to lose, and takes the offer, reasoning that building is something he's always loved and that this might be a real way to get his friends back.

Before leaving for the temple, he writes a lighthearted-but-telling goodbye note for the North, half-joking about being eaten by a monster if he's gone more than two weeks, closing with an insult aimed at Juan specifically ("Juan es un pendejo") that reads more like a private ritual than an actual complaint.

At the site, lightning begins striking as he builds, closer with each strike; what emerges from the temple isn't the mysterious "Fatal Error" entity he'd been chasing, but **Dark Cucurucho** — Cucurucho's voice, badly distorted. The exchange that follows is a genuine binding ritual, not a bit: loyalty, willingness to die, and finally Foolish's soul, held in exchange for the temple's completion. Foolish tries to keep it light with a nervous joke that visibly doesn't land — Dark Cucurucho has no apparent sense of humor — before being told plainly that his friends won't see him again for weeks, though for him it will feel immediate, and that a replacement has already been arranged for his absence. The scene ends there, mid-lightning-strike, deliberately unresolved. [CANON FACT]

## Important dialogue

> **Foolish:** "Oh.. Okay.."

> **Dark Cucurucho, closing:** "it will be weeks before your friends ever see you again. But for you, it will be immediate. Do not fret, I have a replacement for you in your absence!" ... "Good. Time to go."

## Consequences

- Foolish's soul is now bound to Dark Cucurucho pending the temple's completion — a major status change, arguably the single biggest stakes-raiser in the tracked timeline so far. See updated [Q!Foolish](../03-character-sheets/q-foolish.md) status.
- Introduces **Dark Cucurucho** as a distinct entity from Cucurucho — related in voice/appearance, but not established as the same being. Kept separate rather than merged, pending more information.
- "A replacement... in your absence" is an open, ominous thread — who or what stands in for Foolish at the North while he's gone is not yet known.
- Foolish's line "not my first time" (re: dying) lands very differently once you track how many times he's already died in this timeline (EV-0023, the staged death in EV-0051) — a pattern of treating his own mortality as expendable currency, worth being deliberate about in prose.
