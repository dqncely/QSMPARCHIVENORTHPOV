## Summary

Juan discovers Tina cut up his clothes (his only pair of pants into short shorts, a green button-down into a crop top) while he was away — and, still drunk, puts them on and furiously summons Foolish to see. What starts as genuine anger becomes something more charged: Foolish can't stop laughing, then can't stop looking, and notices — without commenting on it — a small butterfly tattoo on Juan's lower torso. The next morning, Foolish returns to Juan's office partly hoping to see the outfit again; instead finds him back in an earlier, similarly revealing Purple Wednesday costume, and has to consciously stop himself from dwelling on it.
