## Summary

The North's residents confront Ash over Estupidog's death. Ash admits to killing him because Tubbo asked him to, saying he's done too much for the Regime to question its orders. Ash then asks Juan the state of North/Regime relations; Juan says it's bad, precisely because of Estupidog's death, and Aldo tells him to prepare for war. Tension escalates: Aldo detonates a mine as a warning (Ash stays calm), then a physical fight breaks out — Ash attacks Juan directly while Juan flees; Foolish attacks Pac (The North's "Fofoquero"), enraging Ash; Aldo clashes with Ash, and it becomes Ash vs. Aldo-and-Juan. Ash insists throughout that he only wants peace. When Aldo tries to withdraw, Ash pursues him, demanding he say "Long live the Regime" to be let go; Aldo refuses, the fight resumes, and Aldo ends up injured with items stolen by Ash. [CANON FACT]
