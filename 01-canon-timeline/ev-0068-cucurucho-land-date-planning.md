## Event

Immediately after Graf's first date, Juan and Foolish scout Cucurucho Land as the location for the next one. They repeatedly insist the park is terrible while enthusiastically riding attractions, playing games, laughing, and inventing excuses to repeat the roller coaster. The interaction is easy and reciprocal in a way Juan's conversation with Graf was not, but calling it a date would be interpretation rather than canon.

Graf arrives while they are still together. Juan and Foolish jointly pretend they have not seen each other all day, fail to make the lie convincing, and turn Juan's attempted goodbye into a group hug. After Juan leaves, Foolish explores the Nether and Polish Cave with Graf. Graf tells him the first date went well and that Juan requested another. Foolish outwardly congratulates him and learns that Graf's intentions are sincere; when Foolish jokingly calls Juan a scumbag, Graf defends him seriously.

## Consequences

- Cucurucho Land is selected for the second Graf date.
- Foolish agrees to remain nearby with a Juan disguise as backup.
- Foolish can no longer dismiss Graf as an obviously unsuitable rival; he recognizes Graf as thoughtful, capable, and sincere. [MY INTERPRETATION based on supplied POV]
- Graf remains unaware that Juan intended to reject him and that Foolish watched/coached the first date.
