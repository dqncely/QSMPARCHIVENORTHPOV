## Summary

Foolish finally shows Juan the temple in full — the actual physical structure from [EV-0055](ev-0055-dark-cucurucho-ritual.md), now confirmed to be nearing completion, with Juan established as effectively the first person let in on the full story (Pierre only saw the location in passing, helping them get there). Foolish walks him through the whole arc: the mysterious book and coordinates found while camping, the ten-plus-hour journey out, the instructions to build sky-to-bedrock, and Dark Cucurucho's appearance in place of the "fatal error" he'd expected. They adopt Star Wars code names for secrecy (Dark Cucurucho as "Palpatine," Foolish as "Obi-Juan," playing off Juan's name), building on their established habit of using movie talk as cover for real conversations.

The mood shifts hard when Foolish mentions the soul risk — Juan's promised calm evaporates instantly, and he flatly rejects the plan, wanting to find an alternative rather than let Foolish go through with it. Foolish argues it's the only real shot the North has ever had at actually fighting the Federation. They talk through darkly comic contingencies (giving away someone else's soul instead, Foolish becoming mortal and getting "killed later" to free himself) before landing, unresolved but calmer, on a working plan: finish the temple, gain Dark Cucurucho's power, use it against the Federation to get Aldo back. They agree to keep it limited — themselves, then eventually Tina, Vegetta, and Aldo — using the Star Wars framing as their actual cover story if anyone asks. [CANON FACT]

## Consequences

- **This reverses the earlier "Juan doesn't know about the pact" status.** As of this event, Juan is fully informed — and immediately, instinctively opposed to Foolish paying with his soul. See [REV-0010](../11-continuity-tracking/rev-0010-juan-learns-dark-cucurucho-pact.md), which updates [REV-0007](../11-continuity-tracking/rev-0007-juan-disappearance-circumstances.md)'s earlier framing.
- A slight tension worth flagging rather than resolving: [EV-0055](ev-0055-dark-cucurucho-ritual.md) has Dark Cucurucho state the soul is already "bound... until my temple is complete," while this scene frames the soul-loss as still conditional/future ("once the temple gets built"). Read as bound-now-but-not-yet-permanently-forfeited; not definitively reconciled.
- Establishes the Star Wars codenames as an ongoing device, reused in [EV-0065](ev-0065-hiatus-week-away-and-return.md).
