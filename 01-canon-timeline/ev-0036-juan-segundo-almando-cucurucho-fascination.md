## Summary

Juan is named "Segundo Almando" (Second in Command) by Vegetta — one of the earliest formal North titles, earned through heavy early labor (collecting sandstone/arenisca, hollowing the mountain for the mansion). Around the same period, the household hierarchy fills in (Aldo generalísimo, Molly princess, Roier prince), and Cucurucho — an indifferent, mysterious pet-adjacent figure — catches Juan's attention specifically. Juan reads a gesture (Cucurucho bringing him a flower) as meaningful despite Cucurucho's uniform indifference toward everyone. Tina and Foolish read this as a "malformed obsession"; Juan assumes that's jealousy on their part. [MY INTERPRETATION — pending confirmation]

## Consequences

- Establishes the origin of Juan's Cucurucho fixation as predating the Robleis impersonation ([EV-0037](ev-0037-robleis-cucurucho-first-date.md)) — the flower gesture that started it may itself have been from the real Cucurucho, or may already have been Robleis. Not resolved.
- Establishes Juan's title and physical labor history, useful for characterization (see [Q!Juan](../03-character-sheets/q-juan.md) writing notes on his build).
