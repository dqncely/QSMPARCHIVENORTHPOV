## Summary

Foolish meets with Juan and Aldo in Juan's office and recounts what happened to him earlier: he found a letter inviting him to a secret party, attended, ended up in a small room where two entities took him to a laboratory and extracted his DNA. Juan and Aldo don't believe him, suggesting he may have been under the effects of some substance and hallucinated it. To prove himself, Foolish offers to take them to the room — but on arrival, the room no longer exists, reinforcing their skepticism. Both conclude Foolish needs rehabilitation. [CANON FACT]

## Consequences

- Sets up "rehabilitation" as a recurring concern about Foolish specifically, feeding into the later group rehabilitation meeting ([EV-0027](ev-0027-north-rehabilitation-meeting.md)).
- Whether Foolish's account is true (a real DNA-extraction incident, possibly Federation-related — see [EV-0030](ev-0030-federation-recruitment-arc.md)) or a hallucination is left genuinely open by the source. Not resolved here.
