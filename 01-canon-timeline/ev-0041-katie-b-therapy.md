## Summary

Juan and Foolish are sent to a forced "relationship therapy" session with Katie B., a therapist running the practice as a side business. The session immediately derails: they fight over which side of the couch to sit on, talk over the talking-stick rule, and — when asked to say something nice about each other — mostly can't, defaulting instead to a long list of grievances (the copied office, Purple Wednesday absence, the bee/ice prank war, Estupidog's death and the "war" it started). Katie tries increasingly desperate techniques (eye contact, saying "I love you," bonding over complimenting her instead), none of which land as intended, before ending the session insisting real progress was made and billing them for diamonds.
