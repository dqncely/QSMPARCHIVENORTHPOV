## Summary

Juan, cast finally off and dressed for adventure, meets Foolish (and, unexpectedly, Mike) at a new park near spawn before heading out — a swing ride goes briefly wrong given the previous night's drinking, and a runaway bit about naming things after yourself ("a Foolish," "a brown") nearly gets away from all three of them before Juan derails it with an oblivious double entendre. At the actual campsite, Juan recognizes the general area from an unrelated earlier outing with Multi, and the two investigate a nether portal, inexplicably surrounded by Brazilian flags, that turns out not to work.

What follows is Foolish's full "camp counselor" routine — equal parts genuine care and deliberate, escalating spookiness about "the fatal error," undercut by real explosions and lightning that keep interrupting his reassurances mid-sentence. Juan's fear is real and immediate (openly invoking the kidnapping at one point) but he stays, largely because Foolish frames everything around sticking together. A second attempt to raise the "666 thousand coordinates" thread — this time from Mike, unprompted — gets deflected by Foolish a second time, noticed by Juan but not pushed. The night includes a real zombie mistaken for the entity, a well with an unexplained skeleton in it, and Foolish's visible, uncharacteristic perfectionism fixing a creeper-blast hole in one of his builds — a level of care Juan's rarely seen from him. By morning, both are teasing each other again, the fear from the night before folded back into their usual bickering. Eventually, after Mike leaves (to both Juan's and, unknowingly, Mike's private relief), they lose their full inventories at the camp and — independently, without discussing it — reach for the exact same solution to get back safely: a parachute. Small, unremarkable, and exactly the kind of shared instinct the author flags as significant. [CANON FACT]
