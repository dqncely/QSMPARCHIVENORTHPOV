## Summary

Back in Juan's office — Juan hitting the alarm out of habit even though Foolish arrived right behind him — the two trade full accounts of the weeks apart. Foolish's version: the kill-mission, Juan's kidnapping as leverage to force Aldo's hand, a string of Regime kills the Federation refused to credit because they specifically required Aldo to be the one killing, Aldo's failed self-sacrifice, and then — camping, chasing the "fatal error" entity on the theory that capturing it might give the North actual leverage against the Federation, since they have no real way to fight them directly. Juan's version, delivered as a deliberately "long version… like a movie," lands harder under the joking frame: nine or ten days in a white room, beaten, starved, alone, until a Federation worker abruptly ended it with no explanation.

Both keep circling back to Aldo — visibly brainwashed, chanting "enjoy the island," neither of them able to tell if it's real psychological conditioning or something closer to possession. The conversation moves through practical catch-up (Tina's failed search and unclear condition, new leadership assignments — Alondrissa now second in command, "Sapotter" holding second in command plus, Foolish keeping plus plus) before landing on something neither of them planned to say out loud: Juan admits, for the first time to anyone, that he's genuinely afraid to go outside now. Foolish's response is to immediately offer a solution rather than sit with it — an invitation to his campsite, framed as exposure therapy dressed up as fun, which Juan accepts. [CANON FACT]
