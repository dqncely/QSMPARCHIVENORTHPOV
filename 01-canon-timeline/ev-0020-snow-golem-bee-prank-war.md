## Summary

Foolish gets revenge on Juan for the office flooding by filling Juan's new office with snow golems. In response, Foolish fills Juan's office with bees; when Juan gets home and enters, the bees sting him and chase him through the mansion. Foolish arrives, asks what happened, sees the infested office, and claims he "didn't know [Juan] liked bees" — Juan says he hates them and insists he knows Foolish is responsible; Foolish denies it. Both end up trying to clear the bees, which instead spreads them through the whole house, stinging both of them repeatedly, before they're finally cleared out. [CANON FACT]
