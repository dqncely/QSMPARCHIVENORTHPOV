## Summary

Foolish starts taking more kelpamine than usual and spending more time alone in the forest, ostensibly hunting the entity, actually avoiding sitting with Juan's absence. He brings people along — Aldo, Tina, Mike, at different times — but none of them understand him the way Juan does, per the author, and each visit ends the same way: someone drifts off bored or homesick, someone comments on how much he's drinking, and Foolish insists he's fine, that he comes out here with a clear mind. A running bit forms — "did you hear that?" from Foolish, "no, dude, are you okay?" from Tina — that reads as comedy on the surface and concern underneath. Mike finds empty kelpamine bottles in the grass; Foolish denies they're his, claims he'd save any he had for recycling.

One night, after the group actually encounters the entity together — terrifying enough that even Aldo, who hadn't believed it existed until it attacked Foolish directly, jumps into action — the three of them sit by the fire afterward, trying to stay light. It's here that Aldo and Tina both, independently and without prompting, say plainly how much they miss Juan; Foolish, by contrast, has to be reminded he hasn't even mentioned that Juan was kidnapped in the conversation yet, which visibly shocks Tina. Aldo separately confides real fear about biological weapons the Federation might be using on Juan. Both of them talk about Juan directly and often. Foolish doesn't, not until pushed. Later, alone, Foolish gets a sudden, sharp chest pain while working at the kelp dragon — he assumes, per his totem nature, that someone nearby used a totem of undying, and jokes aloud, to no one, asking who almost died. The author raises an open question here rather than answering it: given the heart exchange from [EV-0043](ev-0043-heart-saga.md) — each of them having eaten half of the other's heart — does Foolish now feel an echo of whatever's happening to Juan? Not resolved; flagged as a live plot thread.
