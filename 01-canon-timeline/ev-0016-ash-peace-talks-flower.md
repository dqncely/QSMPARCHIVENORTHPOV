## Summary

Juan contacts Ash to propose a peace meeting. The exchange is non-violent, aimed at reducing North/Regime tension. Ash questions whether Juan and Foolish genuinely want war, or whether Aldo is really driving the conflict; Juan says they respect the "Supreme General of War"'s decisions. Ash asks if Aldo holds power over Juan or if Juan just fears him. Juan maintains Ash's actions caused many current problems; Ash counters that The North brings its own trouble too, citing Foolish's call to kill the dog and Aldo's mine detonation. Juan denies that framing and says he'll speak to all North members to maintain peace. Ash says Vegetta would likely agree, and both sides need to be aligned. Ash ends the meeting saying he trusts Juan, and Juan gives him a flower he calls "the flower of peace," also asking Ash not to go to The North at that moment. [CANON FACT]
