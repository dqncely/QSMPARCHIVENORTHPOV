## Participants

- **Characters present:** Q!Juan, Q!Foolish, Q!Tubbo, Q!Tina, Graf. [CANON FACT]

## Summary

Juan and Foolish spot Dark Cucurucho in the water — Juan's reaction is immediate panic, evacuating them both to his room; Foolish takes lightning damage in the process. Foolish gives a first, vague explanation (someone he "met once before on the first island," enemy of the Federation or Cucurucho or everyone) before disappearing overnight — the island gets stuck in permanent darkness while he's gone. Juan's fear for him is genuine and specific, and he's visibly frustrated when Tubbo repeatedly minimizes it by citing Foolish's totem immortality — a dismissal Juan takes personally, having lived through his own trauma being waved away the same way. Ominous, glitchy Federation-style chat messages ("Thank you FoolishG!", then a claimed system glitch) appear during this, unexplained. Foolish eventually messages that he's fine and asks Juan to meet at the dragon. [CANON FACT]

## Important dialogue

> **Tubbo:** "He can't die, he's a totem of undying, bestie."
> — [CANON FACT] — a line Juan visibly resents, given how often this exact framing gets used to dismiss real danger/harm around Foolish.
