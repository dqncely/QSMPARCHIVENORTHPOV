## Summary

At the reveal meeting, Foolish explains what he separately knew: the Federation had offered Fariis a new netherite sword in exchange for killing Foolish, meaning the Federation is running both sides — arming the Regime against the North while pressuring the North against the Regime, engineering a war rather than picking a side. Alone with Aldo afterward, the two settle on a grim plan: kill the Regime's members, since getting Juan out directly doesn't seem possible. Aldo raises the risk of provoking a full war; Foolish's answer is flat — so be it, they're already cornered.

Two deaths are staged as part of this: Fariis is "assassinated" by disguised attackers, and shortly after, Foolish himself is reported murdered in the Northern mansion by an unidentified disguised attacker. Both are Aldo in disguise — Foolish lets himself be killed on purpose, so the Regime won't suspect North involvement in either death. [CANON FACT]

Away from Aldo, Foolish's private reaction to the situation slips out in fragments — half-joking, half-grieving, talking to no one at the kelp dragon as though narrating Juan's death as already final: *he believed in me, he's already reached the end, it's like he wanted me to have his job.* He follows this with a joke about how "funny" the kidnapping is, undercut immediately by "pero estoy triste." The author notes he keeps making these jokes but doesn't seem to believe them — and seems to specifically want to say them to Juan's face, not just to himself. ## Important dialogue

> **Foolish, at the meeting:** "the reason he killed me was because the federation told him that if he killed me, they would give him like a new— netherite sword... it feels like the federation is trying to cause a war."

> **Aldo:** "What will we do about Juan's situation?"
> **Foolish:** "I think our only option left is to kill the members of the regime, because I don't think we can get him out of where he is." ... "Well, so be it. We have our backs against the wall right now."

> **Foolish, alone:** "wait, you're right, he believed in me. He knows it's his time, he's already reached the end. They kidnapped him and now he's dead, we did everything we could. It's like he wanted me to have his job."
