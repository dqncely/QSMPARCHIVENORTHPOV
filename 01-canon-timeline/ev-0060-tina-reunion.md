## Summary

Juan learns Tina's back and rushes to meet her; the reunion is loud, immediate, and mutual. Juan notices her outfit has changed — away from the look she'd usually wear for Aldo's benefit, toward something casual tied to her own interests (a football/soccer jersey for her favorite team). They catch each other up quickly on the new normal: Juan's spontaneous new identity as an artist, having opened a shop mid-recovery seemingly on a whim; Tina's fight with Aldo the day before. Both circle back to Aldo and to Tina's new role as Generalísima before the conversation trails into simple relief — everyone who'd gone missing is, in some form, accounted for again. [CANON FACT]
