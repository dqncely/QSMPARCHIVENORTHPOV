## Summary

Foolish regains consciousness a day before this scene, in grass near a church, with no memory of how he got there — only his mission. After a day back at the temple, he finally messages Juan in chat, prompting a brief flurry of disbelieving private messages before their actual reunion, which happens almost by accident: Foolish walks in on Juan mid-conversation with Multi. The hug is immediate, mutual, and genuinely startles Juan — a real physical relief neither of them fully performs for an audience this time. Multi, forgotten mid-embrace, brings up having seen Foolish at the wedding acting strange; Foolish scrambles for a cover story ("overwhelmed by emotion") that Juan flatly contradicts, and the two of them dissolve into shared, contagious laughter recapping how bad the wedding actually was — a strong, immediate confirmation that whatever else has changed, their specific chemistry hasn't. The same pattern repeats through a string of unrelated bits (Multi's "homeless guy" question turning darkly comedic once it's revealed his workers were killed; an invisible assailant attacking Juan over a note defending Katie B.'s honor) — each time, Foolish's laughter triggers Juan's, and vice versa, feeding a loop neither can fully explain to Multi.

Multi also, carefully, tests whether Foolish is Federation-affiliated; Foolish deflects instantly and smoothly ("I tried to get a job there, but it went bad"), Juan backs him without hesitation. Multi separately notes something strange in Foolish's lab — a "white monster" he apparently "created," which Foolish claims instead to have "kidnapped" (almost certainly the entity from Dark Cucurucho's temple, not clarified further here). Don arrives shortly after Multi leaves, and the conversation shifts to Multi's own lab — which Juan, unprompted, reveals he's actually visited, wearing a hazmat suit, around "green and grey" nuclear-adjacent equipment, with no memory of the exact location. Don and Foolish both react with real alarm; Juan, still not registering the danger, can only explain that he went on the day of an argument with Foolish over missing Purple Wednesday — the same fight had apparently kept him from mentioning the lab visit at all until now.
