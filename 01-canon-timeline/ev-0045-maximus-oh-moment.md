## Summary

Juan, having been told about Foolish's Federation double-agent plan first (before Aldo or Tina — see [EV-0030](ev-0030-federation-recruitment-arc.md)), defends Foolish to a skeptical Maximus — a fellow QSMP1 veteran who deeply distrusts the Federation and, by extension, Foolish. Juan insists Foolish isn't a traitor, listing his flaws at length ("not the smartest, not the most handsome... completely irritating, stupid, crude") while still calling him fundamentally trustworthy. Maximus suggests, directly, that Juan's real feelings for Foolish are the source of his "irrational hatred" toward him. Juan dismisses this outright — then, alone afterward, finds the accusation replaying in his head, and has to admit to himself it makes more sense than he'd like. ## Consequences

- This is the first point in the tracked timeline where Juan's feelings for Foolish are named outright, by anyone — and Juan's internal reaction (denial aloud, quiet admission alone) marks a real turning point rather than a flat "no."
- Reveals significant new backstory: Foolish did something comparable to this Federation plan on QSMP1 — went undercover, ended up with "blood on his hands," and was even the Federation's "employee of the month." Foolish himself draws the comparison to Juan's own "office simulator" habit. See [REV-0008](../11-continuity-tracking/rev-0008-foolish-qsmp1-federation-history.md).
- Confirms the order in which Foolish disclosed the Federation plan: Juan first, then Aldo and Tina.
