## Summary

Juan discovers, via a QSMP TCG card, that his encounters with "Cucurucho" were never with the real Cucurucho — they were Q!Robleis in disguise, an attempt to comfort Juan after the real Cucurucho left him hanging (see [EV-0007](ev-0007-juan-awaits-cucurucho.md), [EV-0008](ev-0008-cucurucho-appears-off-robleis-vanishes.md)). Juan had believed there was mutual interest between them — something that, per this reveal, never existed with the real Cucurucho. He even believed *he* had ended things after Cucurucho's attacks on The North. Realizing it was all fabricated, Juan calls an urgent, visibly shaken meeting with present North members — some try to console him, others laugh. Roier suggests therapy with Melissa; unable to reach her, he recommends Abueloier instead. Abueloier convinces Juan to talk to Robleis directly before asking Vegetta to have him removed from the house. Juan is left heartbroken, having discovered much of what he believed he'd lived through wasn't real. [MY INTERPRETATION — pending QNN confirmation]

## Consequences

- Resolves the long-running Cucurucho ambiguity (see [Cucurucho](../03-character-sheets/cucurucho.md)) — confirms the impersonation theory.
- Major emotional blow for Juan; directly relevant to his romantic/trust history and how it might color his approach to Foolish. See [REV-0005](../11-continuity-tracking/rev-0005-cucurucho-is-robleis.md).
