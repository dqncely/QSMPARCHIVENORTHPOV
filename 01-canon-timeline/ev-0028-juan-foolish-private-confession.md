## Summary

As everyone's leaving, Juan asks Foolish to stay a moment. He waits until Graf — who thanks them warmly in Polish — is out of earshot, then tells Foolish, plainly and in his best English, that he's a good friend: that he likes living and working with him, and enjoys their time together. Foolish, visibly pleased, responds in broken Spanish ("Y yo tambien disfruta tu?"), asking Juan to check if he said it right; Juan corrects him ("Disfruto tu compañía"), and the moment turns into a small language-teasing bit as Foolish tries and fails to repeat "compañía" correctly. Foolish immediately threatens (teasingly) to tell the others what Juan said; Juan protests; they part with Foolish heading off to work, still laughing. [CANON FACT]
