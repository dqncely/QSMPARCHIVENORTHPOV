# EV-0037 — The first Cucurucho "date" — actually Robleis in disguise

## Identity

- **Event ID:** EV-0037
- **Date:** Unknown — author states this predates the QNN-dated [EV-0007](ev-0007-juan-awaits-cucurucho.md)/[EV-0008](ev-0008-cucurucho-appears-off-robleis-vanishes.md) pair (2026-03-19). Original full transcript corrupted/lost; only a fragment survives.
- **Chronological position:** After [EV-0036](ev-0036-juan-segundo-almando-cucurucho-fascination.md); before EV-0007/EV-0008.
- **Certainty level (overall):** Confirmed as reported by the author, though the surviving transcript is only a fragment of a longer original.

## Participants

- **Characters present:** Q!Juan, Q!Robleis (as "Cucurucho"). [CANON FACT, per author]

## Summary

Juan sets up a date with Cucurucho and waits all day — sending Robleis to check on the delay, singing/talking to himself through the night, still waiting into the next morning. When "Cucurucho" finally arrives, it's Robleis in disguise, who — per the author — took on the role originally just to spare Juan's feelings after being stood up, then found himself unable to back out. Juan, overjoyed, proposes marriage during the date; Robleis-as-Cucurucho, unsure how to respond and unwilling to break character, accepts. Juan gets the intimate evening he'd hoped for. Per the author, this is the actual origin of "Juan and Cucurucho" as a perceived relationship — one other North members also believe involves the real Cucurucho. [CANON FACT, per author]

## Detailed sequence of events

1. [CANON FACT] Juan sends Robleis to check on Cucurucho's whereabouts, mentioning he recently changed rooms.
2. [CANON FACT] Juan waits alone for hours, moving from nervous anticipation to self-directed frustration ("qué pinche idiota") to renewed hope as morning comes.
3. [CANON FACT] "Cucurucho" (Robleis) arrives; Juan is overjoyed, treats the late arrival as forgivable.
4. [CANON FACT] Robleis, in character, responds to most of Juan's questions with flat, minimal answers ("Yes," "Yes," "No," "…Enjoy the Island."), notably non-committal and strange, but Juan doesn't register this as suspicious.
5. [CANON FACT] Juan delivers an emotional, committed speech about wanting to move past caution and skepticism about love, and proposes marriage.
6. [CANON FACT] Robleis, having gone too far to break character, accepts.
