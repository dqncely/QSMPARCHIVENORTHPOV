# EV-0078 — Quackity accepts Dark Cucurucho's bargain

## Identity

- **Event ID:** EV-0078

- **Certainty level:** Confirmed at recap level; Dark Cucurucho's claims about kinship and technology are character claims, not independently verified facts.

## Event

After Cucurucho arrests Quackity for destroying a Federation statue with Ewron and returns him to Isla Quesadilla, Dark Cucurucho offers escape in exchange for service. He calls Cucurucho his brother and betrayer and says he will remain inside Quackity to observe through him. Quackity accepts. [CANON FACT for the bargain and claims]

Quackity destroys a secret Federation room aboard the original arrival ship and flees to Foolish's temple. Despite Cucurucho ordering him not to help, Foolish helps Quackity escape through the Nether. Federation guards search the temple, place it under surveillance, and install cameras. [CANON FACT]

Quackity later rescues Colmena from a Federation experiment facility, steals the Teleport Universal from Cucurucho's residence, and returns it to Dark Cucurucho. He then proposes carrying an implanted explosive to kill Cucurucho even at the cost of his own life; Dark Cucurucho accepts the proposal. [CANON FACT]

## Character consequences

- Quackity's protectiveness toward other experiments coexists with escalating self-sacrifice. [CANON FACT for actions; MY INTERPRETATION for the pattern]

- Dark Cucurucho now has an infiltrator, the Teleport Universal, and a planned suicide operation. [CANON FACT for possession/plans]
