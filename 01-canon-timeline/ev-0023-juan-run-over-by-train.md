## Summary

Juan is run over by the train, apparently driven by Foolish, in front of Tubbo. Juan dies from the impact — which also prevents him from delivering the helicopters he'd promised Tubbo. Afterward, a ghost claiming to be Juan appears before Tubbo and Foolish, saying he needs 300 netherite (the payment Tubbo owed for the helicopters) to rest in peace. Tubbo appears to pay it. [CANON FACT]
