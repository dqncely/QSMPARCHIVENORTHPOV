## Summary

Juan, role-playing as "Doctor Atomico," attempts to cure a supposedly-dying Abueloier using Foolish's room as his "laboratory." After failed attempts with watermelon and potato, he declares a heart transplant necessary; Foolish volunteers half of his own heart, reasoning his totem/undying nature will let it regrow. Juan performs a comedic "surgery" (cutting "the wrong side" first), successfully removes half of Foolish's heart — then, instead of using it on Abueloier, makes it into a salad. When Foolish finds out, a chase and knife fight breaks out; Foolish gets the upper hand, and in exchange for helping an injured Juan up, extracts a promise that he'll get half of *Juan's* heart in return — then offers to help regrow it at his kelp lab. [CANON FACT]
