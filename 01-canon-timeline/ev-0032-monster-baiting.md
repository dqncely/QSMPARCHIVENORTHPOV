## Summary

Foolish invites Juan monster-hunting — an entity had recently been spotted on the island, and this is Foolish's first time searching for it, having not yet built a campsite. Juan agrees almost instantly, delays briefly to finish work for Aldo, and follows Foolish out still bantering. On the way, their teasing turns pointed: Foolish jabs at Juan's glasses ("you're just stupid") while visibly stumbling over his own words; Juan claims to look like Captain America. In the forest, the conversation turns to Foolish's relationships — Juan needles him about Tubbo ("your new boyfriend"), Foolish redirects to Robleis, and Juan insists (still, at this point) that Cucurucho was his real boyfriend. The hunt itself is a bust — Juan points at a sheep and calls it "the monster." [CANON FACT]

**Given this is the same day as EV-0031**, Juan's insistence here that "Cucurucho was my boyfriend" is worth reading carefully: either this scene happens *before* the reveal that day (still-sincere belief), or immediately after and he hasn't fully processed it yet, or he's deflecting in front of Foolish specifically. Not resolved by the transcript. Also worth noting: this is now confirmed as one of the very last times these two are shown talking before Juan's disappearance.
