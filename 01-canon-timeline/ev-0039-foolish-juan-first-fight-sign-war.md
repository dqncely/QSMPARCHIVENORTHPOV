## Summary

Their first real fight, explicitly framed by the author as an origin point: Juan calls Foolish into his office over Foolish having changed his own room's name-sign to read "Second in Command" without authorization. What starts as bureaucratic mock-seriousness escalates into Foolish deliberately baiting Juan by breaking office window panels, and Juan retaliating by "demoting" him further and further in an improvised bit — third in command, fourth, fifth — while actually yelling. Neither character realizes in the moment that this exact pattern (rank-based escalation, mutual yelling, property damage) will repeat "countless times" going forward. [CANON FACT]

## Detailed sequence of events

1. [CANON FACT] Juan calls Foolish in over the sign change, initially performing bureaucratic seriousness.
2. [CANON FACT] Foolish plays along, escalating with mock apologies.
3. [CANON FACT] Juan "demotes" Foolish to third in command as a bit.
4. [CANON FACT] Foolish breaks a glass panel in mock outrage, deliberately testing how far the bit will go.
5. [CANON FACT] Juan escalates the demotion to fourth, then fifth in command as Foolish keeps breaking panels and yelling.
6. [CANON FACT] Foolish gets the last word ("that dog still ranks higher than you") and storms out; Juan orders him out and follows anyway.
