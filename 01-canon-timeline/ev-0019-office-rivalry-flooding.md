## Summary

Foolish receives his "second in command plus" promotion and, without telling Juan first, builds himself an office that's an exact replica of Juan's — down to the pictures on the wall, except the side door leads to a sheer drop outside the mansion instead of to a bedroom. He's also wearing an outfit that's a near-copy of Juan's, using an actual shirt of Juan's that barely fits him. Juan discovers all this while walking in on what he thinks is still a private meeting with Ishan, and reacts with escalating (but not fully serious) fury — breaking a glass panel, threatening to kill Foolish in his sleep — while Foolish needles him further by claiming ownership of the room, the "twin" outfits, and even a framed photo of the two of them that Foolish has but Juan doesn't. [CANON FACT]

## Detailed sequence of events

1. [CANON FACT] Foolish interrupts what looks like a meeting between Juan and Ishan; it's revealed they were discussing Foolish's promotion.
2. [CANON FACT] Foolish announces his new rank ("second in command plus"); jokes with Ishan about hierarchy (not above Tina, "above a frog").
3. [CANON FACT] Juan, pleased about the promotion, asks about the office Foolish built — Foolish leads them there.
4. [CANON FACT] Juan discovers the office is an exact copy of his own, down to the pictures — and that the "hallway door" leads to a fatal drop instead.
5. [CANON FACT] Juan notices Foolish is wearing an outfit that mirrors his own, including an actual shirt of Juan's, stretched to fit.
6. [CANON FACT] Escalating argument: Juan breaks a glass panel by accident; Foolish deliberately provokes further; Juan threatens (jokingly, but loudly) to kill him in his sleep.
7. [CANON FACT] Foolish points out a framed photo of the two of them that only exists in his copy of the office.
8. [CANON FACT] Ishan comments that the two "look like twins"; Foolish claims (probably falsely) they bought matching pants by coincidence.
9. [CANON FACT] Juan tries to resume his earlier meeting with Ishan in the wrong chair, realizes his mistake, and reasserts rank ("second in command plus plus") to reclaim his usual seat.
