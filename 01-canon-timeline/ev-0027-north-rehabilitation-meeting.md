## Summary

The North holds a group retreat around a beach campfire, addressing accumulated internal tensions — the sense that recent conflicts have piled up too fast. Each member takes a turn sharing sincere-ish sentiments about the others. Graf and Aldo go first; Juan follows, offering genuine, thoughtful compliments to Graf, Aldo, and Molly — then, reaching Foolish, freezes and deflects into an oddly flat comparison: "you are like an orange.. you're orange and you give vitamin c.. that's all." Foolish plays along ("that really meant a lot"). When it's Foolish's turn, he offers real vulnerability to Graf, Aldo, and Molly, then a more guarded but still warm note to Juan about their live-in, work-in dynamic — ending on a deliberately deflating joke ("please take back your camel").
