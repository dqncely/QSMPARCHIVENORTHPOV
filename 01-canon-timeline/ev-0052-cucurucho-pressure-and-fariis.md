## Summary

Cucurucho escalates the pressure on Aldo directly. He leaves Juan's confiscated teddy bear as a sign reading "See you Soon" — found first by Foolish and others, who dismiss the idea it's specifically Juan's, then by Aldo, who recognizes it as Cucurucho's and connects it (without full understanding) to his task. In a follow-up meeting, Cucurucho shows Aldo video proof that Juan is really being held, hands him a "Penetrating Strike" enchantment meant specifically to help him kill Ash, and sets a Monday deadline. When Aldo asks that Juan not be harmed and demands to see him, Cucurucho's answer is a wordless, unsettling gesture — holding up the teddy bear and saying "here he is" before leaving without explanation.

Fariis, who'd been spying on this exchange, is caught and killed by Cucurucho on the spot — his belongings confiscated. When the real Fariis (evidently returning after the fact, distinct from his earlier staged death) asks Aldo about his missing items, Aldo blames the Federation and denies working for them when pressed on what Fariis overheard — though Fariis remains visibly unconvinced on both counts.
