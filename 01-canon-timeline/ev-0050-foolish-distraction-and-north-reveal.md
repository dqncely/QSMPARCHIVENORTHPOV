## Summary

At first, nothing seems wrong. Foolish goes back to hunting the entity, theorizes that being alone might draw it out, camps with Mike (who gets, or pretends to get, possessed), and ends up swept into a chaotic group encounter at spawn with Molly, Tina, Alondrissa, and Senpai Spider, whose "girls' night" ritual gets interrupted by the entity chasing them all down. Foolish is genuinely baffled that it targets everyone but him. The distraction is real enough that nobody connects Juan's absence to anything sinister until the next morning — though Aldo and Tina have already started noticing and comparing notes before Foolish catches up.

Foolish's own read on Juan's absence shifts gradually: laziness, then confusion (Juan almost never leaves the mansion, let alone skips work entirely), landing eventually on "maybe we're just missing each other" rather than anything more alarming. He keeps half-joking about it publicly — visiting Juan's empty office to leave a photo of the two of them instead of the prank he'd originally planned, telling people Juan's being lazy — while privately, per the author, not actually believing it. When Pac and Mike suggest Juan might have gotten sick of the job, Foolish agrees out loud but doesn't mean it: *abandon work? never.* [CANON FACT for events/dialogue; MY INTERPRETATION for the private disbelief]

The truth arrives at a meeting Aldo calls with whoever he can find — Roier, Alondra, Foolish. Aldo explains that Fariis overheard him talking with Cucurucho and had to be silenced, then delivers the real news: Juan has been kidnapped, and Aldo has until Monday to kill every Regime member or Juan won't be released. Foolish's reaction is a single flat "…What?", then a slower unraveling as the words land — "They kidnapped Juan!!" — while the others' cross-talk becomes background noise he can't process. His one coherent thought, once he can form one: *he's probably so scared right now.* [CANON FACT]
