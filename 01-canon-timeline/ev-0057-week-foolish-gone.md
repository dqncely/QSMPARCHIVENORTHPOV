# EV-0057 — The week Foolish was gone

## Identity

- **Event ID:** EV-0057
- **Date:** Unknown — roughly a week following [EV-0056](ev-0056-juan-return-day-7.md).
- **Chronological position:** Parallel to the tail end of [EV-0055](ev-0055-dark-cucurucho-ritual.md)'s aftermath.
- **Certainty level (overall):** Mostly author summary; treat as [MY INTERPRETATION] throughout unless noted.

## Participants

- **Characters present:** A soulless "replacement" standing in for Q!Foolish, Q!Aldo, Q!Tina, Katie B., Tomate, Erwoon (venue). [MY INTERPRETATION — pending confirmation]

## Summary

True to Dark Cucurucho's promise in EV-0055, a "replacement" Foolish — his body without him in it — is seen around the server during the week he's actually gone, including at Katie B.'s wedding to Tomate, held at Erwoon's church. Juan attends in full armor (his standing habit now whenever he leaves North territory) and notices immediately that something's wrong: the replacement is motionless, silent, unresponsive to anything Juan says or does, including a deliberate attempt to provoke him by calling him "monster." Juan sits beside him anyway when Federation workers at the wedding make him anxious — comfort offered to what may not even be capable of receiving it.

Aldo, meanwhile, is deteriorating under active Federation brainwashing — repeating "enjoy the island" constantly, a total inversion of his usual character. When Juan finally gets access to him and tries to talk sense into him, Aldo hurts him physically, framing the harm as the natural consequence of Juan wanting to keep fighting the Federation. Tina, shaken by her own reunion with Juan, leaves shortly after to search for Aldo directly. She fails to find him, gets knocked unconscious, and is brought back with a group of "Nachos" — chaotic, destructive Cucurucho-faced entities comparable to the QSMP1-era eggs — who'd also returned and been attacked by Aldo themselves. ## Consequences

- Establishes Juan's ongoing "wear armor outside the North" habit as a lasting trauma response, not a one-time precaution.
- Aldo hurting Juan directly is a serious, unresolved wound in their relationship — not yet addressed as of later tracked events.
- Introduces the "Nachos" as a recurring hazard/nuisance tied to Cucurucho, worth tracking as a recurring element rather than a one-off.
- Sets Juan's explicit priorities for what follows: restore Aldo to Generalísimo, and wait for Foolish.
