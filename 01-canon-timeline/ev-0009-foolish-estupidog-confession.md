## Summary

Foolish talks to Juan about Estupidog's death and ends up confessing something important. He first admits the dog's death was part of a test linked to the Regime, and that he was uneasy about a dog holding a high position in The North's hierarchy. Juan reacts with visible upset, and — suspecting Foolish is still holding something back — resorts to psychological pressure: making Foolish believe Estupidog's ghost is manifesting in the house to demand a full confession. Foolish ultimately confesses to having collaborated with the Regime to kill Estupidog, hoping to secure a promotion. [CANON FACT]
