## Summary

In a conversation with someone the author refers to as "Teddy," Juan sets a hard ultimatum for Cucurucho, then immediately begins softening and reframing it — explaining the waiting itself as proof he tried "hasta el último momento." He articulates his own thesis on love ("Porque cuando yo amo, amo de verdad, cabrón") — for Juan, love is never casual; it's total commitment, even when undeserved. He asks Teddy whether he's ever felt like he loves more than he's loved back, and says that's basically been true his whole life — the root of his later possessiveness and jealousy (not ego, but fear of always giving more). The scene closes with a directly quoted monologue in which Juan resolves never to wait and hope like this again.
