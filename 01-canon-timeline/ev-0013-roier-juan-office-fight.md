## Summary

A serious confrontation breaks out in Juan's office. Juan confronts Roier about whether he'd previously had a relationship with Cucurucho; Roier denies it, saying he was only ever followed by someone he called "osito Bimbo," but never wanted it. Juan then reveals that he and Quackity had previously planned to kill Roier. Roier — recalling Quackity's earlier warnings that someone on the island wanted him dead — reacts violently and attacks Juan. With Missa accompanying Roier, Juan (overpowered) calls for Foolish's help; Foolish intervenes and attacks both Roier and Missa, leaving Missa injured and both unable to stand against him. The fight pauses, but Roier later seeks Juan out again to avenge Missa; Juan flees through the mansion begging for it to stop while Missa watches. Foolish intervenes a second time and stops Roier, closing the conflict for now. [CANON FACT]
