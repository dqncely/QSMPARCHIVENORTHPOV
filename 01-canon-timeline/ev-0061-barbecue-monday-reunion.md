## Summary

The North's first Barbecue Monday since everyone's return, held on the rooftop cookout/karaoke setup Juan built up over months — one of the things, per the author, that makes the North feel distinctly like a home rather than just a base. Foolish and Juan's old drinking-pace rivalry resumes immediately, alongside the usual bickering (Foolish's dubious "murderer horse" meat, a mock-argument over who's "helping" whom by running around before eating). The night moves into karaoke: Juan opens with an intense Spanish love song that reads, at least to Tina, like it might be about Aldo — Juan insists it's just "about love," twice, more firmly than the moment strictly requires. Mike and Pierre each take turns (a Spanish song, a deliberately crude comedic French one); Foolish leans into his usual exaggerated non-singing, reliably cracking Juan up; Tina closes the night with Foolish and Juan both stepping in to carry her through the chorus when her voice gives out. [CANON FACT]

Afterward, alone with Tina and Foolish, Juan lays out a genuinely unserious rescue plan for Aldo — disguises, a staged "we hate you" scene meant to bait him back into defiance — allegedly "340 parts" long, floated at a point where all three are visibly drunk. Once Tina leaves for bed, Juan and Foolish end up together on the couch in Juan's room, neither ready to stop being near the other despite having spent the entire day together already; Foolish helps clean cast residue off Juan's newly-freed arm, and both say things that night neither will remember clearly the next morning. [CANON FACT]

## Important dialogue
