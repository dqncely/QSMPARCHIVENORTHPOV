## Summary

Foolish, upset that Juan told Maximus about the Federation plan without waiting even a day, argues that Maximus — the "most anti-Federation guy" around — makes the situation harder, not easier. Juan apologizes but doesn't fully back down on his reasoning. Foolish, frustrated, throws Juan's kept half-heart (see [EV-0043](ev-0043-heart-saga.md)) back at him and storms out muttering; Juan follows and throws it back rather than keep it. The tension breaks into something quieter and harder to name before Foolish deflects into a joke, demanding thirty emeralds for it instead. [CANON FACT]
