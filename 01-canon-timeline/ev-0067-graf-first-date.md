## Event

Juan finishes `El Grifoolish`, a portrait of Foolish inspired by *The Scream*. Foolish is the first customer to receive an original from Mi Arte and reacts with uncomplicated delight. A secret-admirer letter then arrives, directing Juan to the Polish train station at four. The setup directly echoes the false date used to kidnap him, triggering immediate fear. Foolish agrees to accompany him invisibly and watch from a distance.

Graf is waiting at the station. The Federation appears to have recovered and delivered a letter Graf wrote but discarded. Graf slowly leads Juan toward recognizing him as the admirer, eventually answering Juan's direct question—"Graf yo te gusto?"—with a nod. Juan is genuinely shocked and flees to Foolish under the pretext of using the bathroom.

Juan tells Foolish he has only ever viewed Graf as a friend and wants to be honest without hurting him. Three times, however, he cannot deliver the rejection. He talks about weather, invents repulsive dietary habits, and retreats to Foolish for coaching. On the final attempt he tells Graf that love has hurt him before and asks for a second date "to be sure." Graf turns Juan's proposed December 2028 date into the next day.

This is not evidence that Juan reciprocates Graf's feelings. It is evidence that Juan's fear of hurting someone—and his own unresolved fear of love—prevents him from setting the boundary he explicitly intends to set.
